Is $BP the Next BNB? The $100M Market Cap Sleeping Giant You’re Ignoring

发表于2026-08-09|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @MadLukas86（Mad Lukas 🎒） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Historical cycles have proven that centralized exchange (CEX) tokens can deliver multi-bagger returns during growth phases—look no further than $BNB’s journey from ~$0.15 to a ~$900 ATH, or the massive historical expansions of $OKB and $CRO.

If you are looking for the next high-conviction, asymmetrical CEX play for the medium to long term, $BP (@Backpack) stands out as a prime candidate

Here is why $BP is uniquely positioned for outsized growth:

1. Extremely Attractive Valuation Gap

  • Current Valuations: $BP currently trades at a modest ~$97M–$105M Market Cap and ~$390M–$420M FDV (with only ~25% circulating supply).
  • The Peer Gap: Established mid-tier exchange tokens like $KCS trade at ~$915M Market Cap, while $BGB sits at ~$1.15B.
  • The Math: Reaching even KCS-level valuation represents a massive multi-fold upside from current levels.

2. Revolutionary, User-First Tokenomics

Unlike traditional exchange tokens designed to benefit VCs and dump on retail, $BP’s structure is strictly aligned with users:

  • Insiders/VCs: 0% allocation to team, insiders, or VCs at TGE.
  • Community First: 25% airdropped entirely to users.
  • Milestone-Driven Unlocks: The remaining supply only unlocks based on real regulatory, product, or market milestones, or remains locked in the corporate treasury until a post-IPO phase.

3. The Ultimate Staking Utility: Actual Equity

This is the ultimate game-changer: staking BP does not just grant fee discounts. Stakers gain the unique right to convert their BP tokens into actual Backpack equity (up to ~20% of company equity reserved for long-term stakers) in the event of an IPO or acquisition. This bridges the gap between Web3 tokens and traditional finance protection.

4. Massive Catalysts: Leading Solana’s RWA Narrative 🌐

Backpack is aggressively expanding beyond traditional exchange utilities into tokenized Real World Assets (RWAs) via Backpack Securities.

  • Already capturing significant market share in Solana’s tokenized stock trading.
  • Pioneering products like tokenized SpaceX (SPCX) and other equities.
  • Securing major global licenses, including VARA and MiFID II.

While blue-chips like $BNB and $LEO offer stability with limited multiple expansion, $BP offers the highest asymmetric risk/reward profile in the exchange token landscape. Its combination of low entry valuation, absolute user-alignment, and unique equity/RWA catalysts makes it a top-tier asset to hold for the next 2–5 years.

If you have some idle capital, chasing highly speculative memecoins or inflationary altcoins can be an easy way to lose money fast.

$BP offers a more balanced alternative: strong upside potential, but with something real behind it.

Unlike tokens driven purely by hype, $BP is backed by a regulated, revenue-generating platform with real utility and potential equity backing.

That’s what makes $BP interesting to me: less dependent on hype, while still offering the asymmetric upside of getting into a growing CEX ecosystem early.
