CHIPCHIP Monthly Report #8 (2026.8.1–2026.8.31)

发表于2026-09-08|行业观察
|浏览量:

Why Do We Hold Shareholder Meetings?

Our shareholder meeting is a monthly community gathering where we sit down and have an open conversation. CHIPCHIP has been community-driven from the very beginning. It belongs to every player who participates, not just its founders.

Through these meetings, we aim to:

  1. Keep the community informed about project progress
  2. Give the team an opportunity to hear community feedback
  3. Involve the community in decision-making
  4. Reinforce our shared belief that “Every Player Is a Shareholder”

August Project Updates

In August, CHIPCHIP continued improving the MTT experience with updates to tournament information, community partnerships, and player interaction. The team also worked on performance and the experience across devices, addressing reconnection, audio, login, and interface issues.

The Million-Dollar Community Sponsorship Program continued throughout the month. We held five events in partnership with crypto communities including Nuts, 1000X GEM, and GDC, distributing nearly USD 10,000 in prizes. These free tournaments introduced more community players to CHIPCHIP.

We also expanded weekly lobby activities and leaderboards. Together with the ongoing Mad Series, these updates gave players more opportunities to participate and earn rewards.

Product Updates

New Features

  • Added weekly lobby activities with leaderboards by blind level, access to the previous week’s rankings, and an activity entry point at the table.
  • Added pop-up notifications when points airdrops are credited.
  • Split Jackpot pools by blind level, with each table displaying its corresponding pool.
  • Added clickable player avatars and quick phrases to in-game chat.
  • Added adaptive interface scaling across window sizes.
  • Added a “Contact Support” button to invitation pop-ups, linking to Telegram.
  • Added rebuy level indicators to the blind structure at tournament tables.

Fixes and Improvements

  • Enabled chat for MTT spectators.
  • Updated hand history sharing to use replay links and added pagination memory to the sidebar.
  • Improved loading performance for the table sidebar and long lists.
  • Addressed table reconnection issues after backgrounding on iOS.
  • Addressed X avatar loading failures and unresponsive Google login.
  • Improved insurance purchases to prevent repeated-click errors; auto top-up now switches off when funds are insufficient.
  • Adjusted the order of MTT placement and settlement pop-ups and improved player information pop-ups.
  • Fixed missing interactive emoji audio and display issues affecting homepage ads, room lists, and online player lists.

Marketing Activities

Mad Wednesday Tournaments

We held four Mad Wednesday events this month: two online livestreamed tournaments and two major in-person events.

On August 19 and 26, we hosted in-person tournaments in Taiwan and Hong Kong, respectively, featuring crypto KOLs and online personalities. 撒幣哥 won the Taiwan event, while 玄之 took the Hong Kong title. The events attracted more than 3,000 livestream viewers.

Mad Wednesday is a crossover poker reality show hosted by CHIPCHIP. We invite crypto KOLs, poker stars, and livestream personalities to compete alongside CHIPCHIP community players, with every event livestreamed. Each winner also receives prize money and an exclusive champion’s keepsake from CHIPCHIP.

CHIPCHIP Million-Dollar Community Sponsorship Program

We held five events under the Million-Dollar Community Sponsorship Program this month, partnering with popular crypto communities including Nuts, 1000X GEM, and GDC. These free tournaments distributed nearly USD 10,000 in prizes.

Through continued community partnerships, we aim to give more players the opportunity to experience CHIPCHIP MTT for free and compete with players from other communities.

The CHIPCHIP Mad Series Continues

We prepare a range of prizes for players every day. In designated activity rooms, the top 10 players by daily qualifying turnover at the corresponding stake level receive raffle tickets, with 25 tickets distributed each day.

The operations team uses a raffle tool to draw a winning ticket based on the tickets held by those top 10 players. The holder of the selected ticket wins the day’s grand prize. In addition, the top three players at the 1U and 2U levels receive extra rewards ranging from 5,000 to 30,000 points.

CHIPCHIP Weekly Leaderboards

Weekly rankings track both turnover and hands played. The top three players on each leaderboard receive points, which can be redeemed for prizes in the store.

Leaderboard Rewards

  • 1st place: 50,000 points
  • 2nd place: 30,000 points
  • 3rd place: 20,000 points

Leaderboard Data Refresh: Every Sunday at 24:00 (UTC+8).

September Outlook

September will focus on improving the core experience and expanding platform features:

  • Homepage Redesign: Make tables easier to browse and reduce the steps needed to take a seat.
  • Club Features: Advance development to support player organization and game operations for communities and offline clubs.
  • Multi-Table Support: Enable players to participate at and switch between multiple tables.
  • Payment Channels: Add payment options to make deposits and withdrawals more convenient.
  • Mini-Games: Expand entertainment options alongside the core poker experience.
