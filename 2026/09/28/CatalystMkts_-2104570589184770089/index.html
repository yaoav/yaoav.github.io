man made fire

发表于2026-09-28|行业观察
|浏览量:

were you paying attention? did you catch it?

last friday, we announced our launch on Robinhood Chain via X.
attached to the post was a video of a lit match.
a subtle clue for what was about to happen.

57 seconds later, @RobinhoodCrypto reposted it.

1.3 seconds after the repost, a market on catalyst had drained its fuse pool and converted its liquidity to $CATALYST in a single transaction.

17 traders benefited from the catalyst and were immediately airdropped their $CATALYST outcome tokens.

the setup

the market was aptly named FIREPROOF. proof of fire.

one catalyst: @RobinhoodCrypto follows, replies to, mentions, reposts or quotes @CatalystMkts_

one outcome: buy $CATALYST

quick mechanics primer in case you haven’t been dialed in until now:

  • fuse. conviction on a catalyst event. trade it or hold it for the fire.
  • fade. the pool against the fuse. earn yield until the catalyst decides.
  • fire. the moment the catalyst resolves. positions convert in seconds.

the FIREPROOF market opened thursday, a whole day before. those who discovered it before friday were rewarded.

FIREPROOF’s fuse pool held $1,048.54 while its fade pool received $671.84 in deposits. and just 24 hours, 43 minutes and 6 seconds after it opened, it fired.

onchain, FIREPROOF’s watcher was the first to react, just 1.4s after the repost from @RobinhoodCrypto. fuse pool drained. outcome tokens purchased and airdropped.

17 FIREPROOF fuse holders were rewarded their share of the 684.79 $CATALYST tokens (purchased at $1.52/token) along with their share of the $671.84 USDC fade pot.

$CATALYST’s price then appreciated +18% in 60s after the repost, +28% by 100s, and +42% over the course of the day.

for a $100 fuse position, this was the payout:

(and despite being first, FIREPROOF’s reaction actually leaned slow according to our benchmarks. trust that the next fire will be even faster.)

the opportunity

attention moves price. it always has.

a follow. a repost. a reply from the right account. tokens have repriced in seconds on less.

catalyst markets allow anyone to react to any event in milliseconds and front run the entirety of the price reaction.

  • when @cobie followed @BasecatOnBase, $BASECAT token appreciated +242%.
  • when @vladtenev followed the founder of $WEN @agilepeter, $WEN appreciated +530%.
  • when @finkd (Mark Zuckerberg) followed @agrippa_muse, $AGIPPA appreciated +2,568%.

you don’t have to be faster than any bots.
the catalyst market is the bot.
and holding the fuse token guarantees your seat on the ride up.

what’s next

20+ new markets are shipping today, and more will ship every day of this week. its quite possible we see another fire before the end of the week.

and the best for last, of course.

soon, some of you will earn the ability to create markets yourselves.
more importantly, you’ll share in the economics of the catalysts you create.

man made fire.
spread the word.
play the game.
