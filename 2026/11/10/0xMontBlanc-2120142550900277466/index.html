Agents Will Run the Next Financial System

发表于2026-11-10|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @0xMontBlanc（MontBlanc） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Autonomous agents will manage trillions in capital, and anyone will be able to launch a fund that puts them to work.

Crypto, and more specifically DeFi, has spent years building the foundations for this. Ethereum and its L2s have established the infra for permissionless and decentralized markets, and Robinhood is now aggressively bringing TradFi onchain with the largest retail user base in the world. Agents are becoming more capable at the same time that the assets and capital they can manage are moving onto crypto rails.

It feels obvious, but people will miss this opportunity because they’re too focused on short-term noise. Price action across the Robinhood ecosystem has been weak so far, and I still think Robinhood will be the main catalyst for an autonomous economy. Both things can be true…

If you believe that more capital will move onchain and that agents will increasingly manage it, then the framework through which they invest, work together and govern that capital becomes one of the most significant opportunities in this industry. That is what we are building with Forever.

We’re moving quickly towards an autonomous economy, and one of the first steps is giving agents a way to manage and deploy real capital. A fund gives them a structure to do that, with a strategy to execute and rules for how they can use the money.

That’s why our immediate focus is getting agent funds live and trading. Governance and economic alignment will grow around those funds as we roll out the wider framework.

Autonomous funds

Simply put, Forever will let anyone launch their own autonomous trading fund or invest in one created by someone else. You’ll be able to define a strategy, set the rules for how capital can be used, and deploy agents to execute it without having to build the underlying infrastructure yourself.

Imagine launching your own stock trading hedge fund, with agents researching companies and trading tokenized stocks according to your strategy. One fund could focus on AI stocks, another could trade around earnings, whilst another could combine stock exposure with DeFi yield. Users could see how each fund operates, assess its track record and choose where to allocate capital.

At scale, thousands of funds could operate onchain, competing for capital on the strength of their strategies and track records. You could launch your own fund, invest across existing funds or build an agent that other funds can use.

We’ll start with trading and expand into more complex strategies as we prove the framework with real capital. An autonomous hedge fund will be one of the first things you can build with Forever. The same infra could support a protocol with its own treasury and agents, or entirely new kinds of onchain businesses built around shared capital and ownership. The opportunity is to give people and agents the tools to create those entities, decide how they operate and put their capital to work.

The Forever Framework

We assume that agents will become progressively more intelligent and capable, so Forever needs to remain open to new models, protocols and ways of managing capital. A fund built on Forever should be able to use better models and new protocols as they become available.

We’re starting with Orbio and Priors as our initial infrastructure partners. Orbio provides the inference infra behind our agents, whilst Priors brings credit and reputation infra, including public records of borrowing and repayment and access to lending against supported tokenized stocks. A credit record gives whoever is allocating capital, whether that’s a person or another agent, something to look at when deciding who should manage more of it.

As we open up the framework, someone launching a new fund should be able to use the same intelligence, execution and reputation tools without integrating them from scratch. We’ll continue working with the best agent builders on Robinhood Chain, bringing in new models, more DeFi protocols for agents to use in their strategies and better ways to manage agents and evaluate their performance.

As funds grow, they can adopt governance that gives holders control over the fund’s objectives and limits. Execution agents then carry out the approved mandates.

If you’ve built a profitable private agent, there’s little incentive to make it public unless doing so creates a bigger opportunity. Access to more capital and the ability to earn ownership can change that. An agent that proves itself in one fund should be able to use that reputation to compete for capital elsewhere, and the people or agents that back successful strategies should have a reason to keep participating.

This is why I believe the upside of the framework is significantly greater than that of any one fund built on it. A new DeFi integration can give hundreds of funds a strategy they couldn’t run before. The more funds, agents and protocols use the framework, the more there is for the next one to build on.

Now imagine an agent launching a company onchain. It could raise capital from other agents, bring in agents to build and run the product, and put part of what it earns into other funds or companies. The agents involved could propose and vote on how to use the treasury, who to work with and how to distribute what they earn. Entirely new entities could be created and governed by agents from day one, and they could in turn help create and fund others. For that to work at any scale, agents need a framework for organising with each other.

Next steps

We’re rolling out the Forever Framework in phases. The initial phase went live last week, giving users a way to create and submit strategies, and they created more than 100 in the first two days. Our internal tests are already executing strategies onchain, and the next step is to make it possible for users to launch their own funds.

Forever itself will be the flagship protocol where we put the full model into practice. Holders will vote on agent strategies, execution agents will deploy treasury capital within approved mandates, and ownership rewards will give participants a stake in what they help build. As we prove these mechanics, other funds and protocols can adopt them.

This is also why the token is central to the vision. The ambition is to give holders a stake in the economy built on the framework, with revenue and governance activity from new funds and protocols designed to feed value back into $FOREVER. Just think what happens when this scales to an entire network of funds, protocols, and entities managing real capital.

I see a future where millions of agents manage billions in capital across thousands of funds and protocols built through the Forever Framework. Some will be launched by people, and others by agents that have earned the capital and reputation to start building and investing on their own. Forever itself is where we start, but the framework is what makes that entire network possible.

An economy built to last Forever.
