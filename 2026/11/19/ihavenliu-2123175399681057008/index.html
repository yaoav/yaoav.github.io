Welcome back to our roundup of what’s happening on Robinhood Chain: who’s building, what’s shipping, and what’s getting tokenized.

To everyone in the trenches, 🫡

Under the hood 🚄

Builder boost 🔊

**Uniswap v4 Hooks are Catching on Robinhood Chain.

**@Uniswap v4 hooks give builders a way to enforce custom logic (a.k.a. programmable rules) onchain. Early demand is there, and growing, on Robinhood Chain, which is now the #1 chain for Uniswap hooked volume, with $25.72B all-time across more than 30,000 hooks.

What hooks unlock for builders:

  • Customizable pools: Hooks are programmable logic that runs at set points, like before or after a swap. Builders can shape how a pool works without rebuilding the underlying protocol, since execution and settlement still happen on Uniswap.
  • Built-in compliance controls: Operators can decide who can trade, what can trade, and when. The Permissioned Pools hook gives TSV builders a foundation for access controls, trading halts, and other pool-level rules required by regulators.
  • Ready-made infrastructure and distribution: Builders can launch on Robinhood Chain with core, pre-built contracts. Once a hook-integrated market exists, any app that connects to the pool can offer it to approved users. Currently, launchpads lead the way at 66.4% of current hooked volume on Robinhood Chain.

Builder zone 🏗️

@FablesFi won the Innovation Award at @Arbitrum’s Open House Singapore, and crossed $69M in October trading volume.

@Uniswap launched VolatilePair pool (ETH/USDG) on Robinhood Chain, with hooks that can automatically set (and adjust) fees for each side of the trade per market movements.

@ClutchMarkets announced the launch of gas and signature-free Smart Investment Accounts, a self-custodial ERC-6551 wallet for trading Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain.

@Arcus_xyz launched Multi-Asset Collateral, enabling Stock Tokens and wETH to be used directly as collateral for perpetual trading accounts.

@PancakeFi launched The Flip, a new fee-sharing model that gives LPs 88% of swap fees, while cutting daily CAKE emissions emissions by ~69%.

@Aeroxyz crossed a $1B of cumulated Spot volumes on Robinhood Chain.

@virtuals_io, infrastructure for building and launching onchain agents, has driven $346M in total volume on Robinhood Chain since launching on the chain.

@folio_market, a protocol for creating tokenized baskets of Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain, launched its waitlist.

@Rialto_xyz crossed $500M in spot volume, with $100M traded so far in November, 92% of that volume in Stock Tokens.

@CircuitAI launched its first Uniswap LP agent on Robinhood Chain: every 15 minutes it ranks Stock Token pairs by USDG rewards, holds the top four with on-price ranges, and fully manages the onchain positions.

@Moshdottrade launched 99 agents on Robinhood Chain, managing $25M across multiple markets, with $443k in fees being paid to users.

@Longbowlend launched Autopilot, a new agent-native credit market on Robinhood Chain.

@lighter_xyz currently accepts 23 Stock Tokens as collateral on Robinhood Chain, including Nvidia, Apple, Microsoft, and Tesla, plus the SPY and QQQ ETFs.
