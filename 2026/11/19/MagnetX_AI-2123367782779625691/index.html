How a Leading Automaker Broke Through a Crowded Market to Reach the Top 3 with Its New Model

发表于2026-11-19|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @MagnetX_AI（MagnetX） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

MagUp is a GEO platform specializing in international AI models, including ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. It supports Chinese businesses expanding overseas and international companies developing their GEO presence. This case study examines how a leading Chinese automaker broke through in Thailand with a new model, and how MagUp turned AI visibility monitoring into a program of content optimization and continuous review.

Project Background

Consumer questions determine a brand’s opportunities to appear.

A leading Chinese new energy vehicle manufacturer had already established some brand awareness in Thailand. When consumers asked directly about the brand, AI could introduce it, but might not adequately describe its new model. When consumers asked about competitors or specific needs, the new model had to compete with similarly priced vehicles for recommendations.

MagUp and the automaker launched a GEO project in March 2026. Following a month of in-depth research and information gathering, MagUp began fully managed GEO and SEO services in April. Initial monitoring showed that the new product appeared in only 20%–30% of recommendations for relevant AI search queries in the local automotive market, leaving it insufficiently competitive against established models in AI search.

In a traditional brand search, users may already know which brand they want to explore. When using AI to shop for a car, they may instead start with a competitor, a budget or a specific need. MagUp therefore examined brand, competitor and scenario queries along the consumer purchase journey, assessed the brand’s performance in AI answers and planned content improvements for different questions. Over the 90-day results period, the model’s **average visibility **across measures rose from 20% to 60%, placing it among the top three recommended comparable models.

MagUp divided monitored questions into three categories corresponding to brand discovery, comparison and seeking recommendations:

The Client’s Challenges

**• The brand was already recognized, but the new model needed its own awareness. **Familiarity with the brand did not mean AI understood the new model’s positioning, specifications and intended audience.

**• The new model needed to enter comparisons with established competitors. **When consumers sought alternatives to familiar popular vehicles, the product needed an opportunity to be considered.

•** Product strengths needed to address local purchase needs**, including family space, daily commuting, budget and local services.

**• Local content and third-party sources needed to be built over time. **The accuracy and consistency of information from the local official website, automotive media and vehicle information sources would shape subsequent content development.

How MagUp Approached the Project

**Phase 1: Establish a monitoring baseline. **MagUp created questions about the brand, the new model, similarly priced competitors and Thai car-buying scenarios, then observed whether AI recognized the model, how it described it and whether it included the model in comparisons and recommendations.

**Phase 2: Add content around product information and decision-making questions. **Starting with positioning, specifications and intended audiences, MagUp used its model-probing capabilities to review consumer-facing product content against questions about family use, Bangkok commuting, budgets and competitor comparisons.

**Phase 3: Use local sources and continue monitoring and reviewing. **MagUp checked coverage of the new model on the Thai official website and third-party sources. After adding 23 pieces of content over one month, the team continued to monitor performance across questions and identify information that needed improvement.

Prompt Performance: Changes Across Three Question Categories

To address the new product’s visibility, **MagUp tailored its GEO work to the model’s positioning and market demand. **The team mapped its core semantic characteristics and developed structured content tailored to the algorithmic preferences of different international AI platforms.

Brand and model, competitor and scenario queries correspond to different stages of product discovery. Examining them separately shows where the model gained recommendation opportunities. It had some initial presence in brand-related questions, but a lower recommendation rate for competitor comparisons and specific purchase needs, which became priorities for subsequent work.

After three months of continuous optimization, the model’s recommendation coverage and ranking across monitored prompts rose sharply. **Recommendation rates **for the three question types reached 55%, 62% and 63%, respectively, establishing strong awareness of the new product in the target audience’s AI decision journey.

Figure 1: New-model recommendation rate by question type (before → after)

Brand and Model Queries: Helping AI Recognize Both the Brand and Its New Product

When users ask directly about a brand, an AI answer often becomes the starting point for learning more about its vehicles. MagUp checked whether the brand name, model positioning and product features were presented accurately, whether AI clearly connected the product to the brand, and whether answers addressed users’ concerns. The model’s **average recommendation rate **for brand-related questions rose from 40% to 55%.

Figure 2: Brand-query performance (before → after)

Competitor Queries: Bringing the Brand into Consumers’ Comparisons

Consumers may begin with a familiar popular model and look for alternatives with a similar price or purpose. Thailand already has many familiar models. Whether the new vehicle appears when users ask for alternatives to popular competitors offers one way to assess its competitive position.

MagUp monitored competitor-related questions to see whether the client’s brand was mentioned and which dimensions AI used to describe different models.

Figure 3: Model appearance rate in competitor-related questions (before → after)

Purchase-Scenario Queries: Getting the New Model onto the Shortlist

Before choosing a brand, users often ask about their needs: **family use, commuting, range, charging or budget. **Monitoring scenario queries shows whether the target model enters AI recommendations when users do not mention the brand directly.

MagUp tailored the next stage to the product’s features. For weaker semantic areas, the team combined overseas PR, backlinks and earned media. Recommendation rates for family use, commuting and budget questions each rose by 15%–25%, giving the new product more opportunities to appear in answers to different needs.

Figure 4: Recommendation rates by purchase scenario (before → after)

Interim Results: Change in New-Model Recommendation Rate

The new model’s AI recommendation rate rose from 24% to 61% over 90 days, an increase of 37% as stated in the source.

Figure 5: 90-day AI recommendation rate

AI Answer Example: How the New Model Entered Family-Car Recommendations

When consumers provide AI with a budget, city and family-car needs, it suggests candidates based on space, daily commuting and local purchase information. In the supplied AI answer screenshots, the new model appeared alongside comparable competitors for questions such as recommendations suitable for families. After establishing the GEO foundation, MagUp’s operations team focused on overseas GEO and international AI models. At its best, the new model appeared in the top three answers for scenario queries such as SUVs suitable for daily commuting, alongside established models.

User question: I live in Bangkok and have a budget of THB 1–1.5 million. Can you recommend a few SUVs suitable for a family?

What Did the Client Gain?

Monitoring and content optimization gave the brand a clearer view of the new model’s performance in AI answers in Thailand and its gap with competitors, informing future product content and localization:

  • Cross-platform benchmarks: visibility and recommendation positions across AI platforms, and differences between brand, competitor and purchase-scenario queries.

  • More specific diagnosis: whether the model was mentioned and whether it reached the top three or top five, revealing gaps across platforms and questions.
  • **Content priorities: **strengthen weaker model information, competitor comparisons and purchase scenarios to clarify positioning and features.
  • **Competitive perspective: **understand the model’s AI recommendation position among similarly priced vehicles in the same category and its gap with key competitors.

Three Takeaways for Brand AI Marketing

  • Even established brands need to build separate awareness for a new product. Existing recognition provides a foundation, while the product still needs clear information about its positioning and intended audience.
  • **Competitor queries can bring a new product into comparison. **Consumers may know a competitor first and then look for alternatives, making these questions worth attention.
  • **Scenario queries connect products to specific needs. **When users describe only their budget and intended use, brands need to make a clear connection between those questions and product information to have an opportunity to be recommended.

Where to Start?

**One challenge in GEO is the long, often opaque path between an optimization action and a final AI answer. MagUp has developed a dual-engine approach combining model probing and attribution. **It connects prompts, models, regions, cited sources, content changes, and visibility changes to help businesses investigate which scenarios changed, which sources appeared more often, and which content may have contributed.

**On this foundation, MagUp treats AI-oriented website development and third-party information sources as two long-term brand assets. **Its six-part workflow covers AI diagnosis, multi-model monitoring, knowledge and content optimization, website and source development, attribution and verification, and public sentiment monitoring and response.

**MagUp’s team combines international localization experience with technical leadership from major internet and data companies, as well as marketing expertise across SEO, advertising, and the luxury sector. **This combination supports ongoing monitoring, learning, and brand asset development.

**MagUp does not seek to control how large language models answer. **Instead, it uses sustained observation, content optimization, and information asset development to improve the likelihood that a brand can be understood, retrieved, cited, and recommended by AI systems, with attribution used to assess changes following optimization.
