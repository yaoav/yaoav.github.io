A Beginner's Guide to HertzFlow Credit

发表于2026-11-13|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @Hertzflow_xyz（HertzFlow | Mainnet Arc🎲） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

HertzFlow distributes Credit to the community from time to time, based on trading fees and campaign contributions. The Credit Market is now live, so eligible users can claim their Credit and use it to trade BTC/USD.

Congratulations to everyone who received Credit. If you did not receive any this time, stay tuned and follow HertzFlow on X for future updates.

Credit cannot be withdrawn. You can use it in two ways.

  • Trade on the Credit Market. Use Credit as collateral to trade BTC/USD. Profits are paid to your wallet in USD1, and losses only burn Credit.
  • Redeem it for fee rebates. Within your fee rebate allowance, redeem Credit for USD1 at a one to one rate, which gets part of your trading fees back.

1. Claim Your Credit

Start here: Claim Credit

Connect the wallet that may be eligible. If you received Credit, the amount appears on the page.

Select Claim, then confirm the transaction in your wallet.

Claim within the Claim Period. Unclaimed Credit is burned and not reissued.

After claiming, your balance appears under Available. Credit committed to an open trade appears under Used for Trades.

2. Trade with Credit

Step 1: Open the Credit Market

Go to: Trade on HertzFlow

Open the market selector and choose the Credit tab. The Credit Market currently supports BTC/USD only. You can identify it by the Credit tag.

Direct link: BTC/USD Credit Market

Step 2: Set Up Your Trade

Select Long if you expect the BTC price to rise. Select Short if you expect the BTC price to fall.

Choose Market to open the trade at the current available price. Enter the Credit amount you want to use under You’re paying, or select MAX.

Under Mode, confirm that Normal is selected, then set your leverage from 1.1x up to 20x.

Higher leverage creates a larger position from the same Credit amount. It also makes the position more sensitive to price movements. Check the position details carefully before continuing.

Step 3: Approve and Open the Position

Before your first Credit trade, select Approve Credit and confirm the transaction in your wallet. This gives the Credit Market permission to use your Credit as collateral.

After approval, select the Long BTC or Short BTC button. Confirm the trade in your wallet.

The Market Order first shows Submitted, then Filled after execution. Your new position appears under Positions.

3. Profit and Loss

If the market moves in your favor, the position can earn a profit. Profit is paid automatically to your wallet in USD1 when you reduce or close the position. There is no claim profits button.

If the market moves against your position, losses reduce the Credit used as collateral. Losses only burn Credit, never your own funds.

Credit used as collateral does not return to Available, even when you reduce or close the position.

4. Another Use: Fee Rebates

Hold Credit and trade in Normal mode on regular markets outside Credit to build a fee rebate allowance. Within this allowance, you can redeem Credit for USD1 at a one to one rate through Claimable Fee Rebates on the Credit page. There is no claim deadline.

In plain words, this lets you get part of your trading fees back. A full guide to fee rebates is coming soon.

5. Good to Know

For more information, read the HertzFlow documentation.
