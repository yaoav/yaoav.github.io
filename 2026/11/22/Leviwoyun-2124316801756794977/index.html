100 Standout Website Footers (Organized by Style)

发表于2026-11-22|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @Leviwoyun（Levi 卧云） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Most footers are an afterthought—just a dump of links at the bottom of the page. These 100 design references show how a page can close with personality, clarity, or a strong call to action.

You’ll find a mix of live websites and concept explorations below, each credited with a link to its original post.

Contents: Illustrated Storytelling (20) · Oversized Wordmarks (20) · Minimal Layouts (8) · Information Architecture (12) · Conversion-Focused CTAs (25) · Nature-Led Atmospheres (6) · Playful Interactions (5) · Brand Storytelling (4)
