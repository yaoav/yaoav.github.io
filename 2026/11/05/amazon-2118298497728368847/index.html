Amazon announces partnership with Uber Eats offering Prime members $0 delivery

发表于2026-11-05|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @amazon（Amazon） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Prime members nationwide can now order from Uber Eats directly on Amazon, potentially saving more than $175 a year*.

Key takeaways

  • Prime members now get $0 Delivery Fee on Uber Eats restaurant orders of $25 or more when ordering right on Amazon.
  • No new app or Uber Eats account is needed. Simply search “Uber Eats” on Amazon to browse restaurants and place an order.
  • To get started, Prime members can get $20 off each of their first two Uber Eats restaurant orders of $25 or more.

Prime members nationwide can now place Uber Eats restaurant orders directly through Amazon.com and the Amazon Shopping app with $0 Delivery Fee on orders over $25. Ordering is simple using existing Amazon account credentials, with no new app or Uber Eats account needed to get started. The new Uber Eats benefit is available to all Prime members starting today, including members of Amazon’s half-price memberships Prime for Young Adults and Prime Access.

The new partnership gives Prime members access to Uber Eats’ vast selection of hundreds of thousands of restaurants nationwide, reaching nearly every household in the U.S. In addition to $0 Delivery Fees, members can also look forward to Prime-exclusive offers available only on Amazon, potentially saving more than $175 per year*—more than the price of an annual Prime membership.

“Whether it’s game night, family night, or date night, Prime’s new Uber Eats membership benefit makes getting dinner delivered even easier,” said Jamil Ghani, worldwide vice president of Amazon Prime. “This is what Prime is all about: unlimited free, fast delivery on a wide selection of products, big savings, fantastic entertainment, and now even more value on restaurant delivery with Uber Eats, all in a single membership.”

“Together with Amazon, we built an experience for Prime members designed to make restaurant delivery feel as natural, convenient, and affordable as ordering anything else on Uber Eats,” said Andrew Macdonald, president and chief operating officer, Uber. “We’re excited to connect our restaurant community with new Prime fans, while giving them a seamless way to enjoy great food from restaurants through Uber Eats.”

Where is Prime’s new Uber Eats benefit available?

The new benefit is available to members coast to coast, from major metro areas like Boston, Atlanta, and Los Angeles; suburban areas like Oak Park, Illinois, and Daphne, Alabama; to rural communities like Boardman, Oregon, and Berkeley Springs, West Virginia; and nearly ten thousand more cities and towns in between. With access to Uber Eats’ extensive selection of hundreds of thousands of restaurants, the options are endless—including national favorites like Wingstop, Chipotle, and Sweetgreen, regional staples like Aga’s Restaurant in Houston and Papalote Mexican Grill in San Francisco, plus a variety of small business restaurants in their own backyard. There is no need to worry about missing out—Uber Eats on Amazon reflects a broad restaurant selection similar to what is available on the Uber Eats app.

How can Prime members get started with Uber Eats on Amazon?

Members can visit www.amazon.com/ubereats or open the Amazon Shopping app and search “Uber Eats” to get started in three simple steps. To activate, log in with existing Amazon credentials, browse from nearby restaurants, and then place an order with existing Amazon payment methods. Amazon will send email and push notifications with updates on delivery status, and then Uber Eats will send a final order confirmation. Every Uber Eats order of $25 or more made on Amazon from a qualifying restaurant has $0 Delivery Fee. To celebrate the launch, for the first 14 days, members can get started with $20 off each of their first two Uber Eats orders of $25 or more. Plus, Prime Visa card members earn 5% back on all Uber Eats orders through December 5, 2026. Eligible Prime membership required.

What’s included with Prime? More convenience, savings, and entertainment

Whether it’s ordering takeout from Uber Eats or tackling a grocery list, Prime makes it all possible. With Prime, households can choose between ready-made burgers and pizza for the main course with Uber Eats, and then round out the meal with fresh ingredients for the side salad or appetizer, thanks to free Same-Day Delivery on orders over $25 from Amazon Grocery.

Prime’s new Uber Eats benefit pairs perfectly with a membership packed with non-stop entertainment as well. Prime Video delivers award-winning movies, shows, and exclusive live sports like Thursday Night Football. The entertainment continues with ad-free Amazon Music listening, cloud gaming with Amazon Luna, free storage with Amazon Photos, access to a monthly Twitch subscription, a broad selection of popular eBooks, audiobooks, comics, and manga, and so much more. And with Alexa+, Amazon’s AI assistant included with Prime, members can discover entertainment, shop, and manage everyday tasks through natural conversation.

Beyond entertainment, Prime is built to save members money and time on everyday needs. Members enjoy everyday low prices and free Prime shipping on 300 million items across 35 categories. When a last-minute need comes up, Prime members in select cities and towns can count on discounts across a variety of ultra-fast delivery choices, including options that arrive in as little as 30 minutes. Members also enjoy fast, free delivery of prescription medications through Amazon Pharmacy, add-on healthcare savings, and everyday fuel savings.

The value from Prime’s benefits adds up quickly. Last year, members saved an average of over $650 on delivery fees and fuel. They also avoided an average of 64 trips to a physical store in 2025, equating to over 55 hours of time saved. Now that $0 Delivery Fees on qualifying Uber Eats orders are a part of Prime membership, members can look forward to saving more time and money on at-home dining.

How can you join Prime?

Anyone can join Prime for $14.99 per month or $139 per year, or start a free 30-day trial if eligible on Amazon. Amazon also offers discounted memberships for higher-education students and young adults ages 18 to 24 with Prime for Young Adults, and for qualifying government assistance recipients and income-verified customers with Prime Access. Prime members can share a broad range of benefits, including restaurant delivery powered by Uber Eats, with one adult in their household through Amazon Family.

*Estimated savings are based on placing the typical number of Uber Eats orders on an annual basis, annualized based on August 2026 order data. Savings are based on the discounts and promotions available to Prime members ordering within Amazon.com or the Amazon Shopping app, compared to being placed on Uber Eats directly without Uber One membership. Savings per order will vary and are not guaranteed. Offers and promotions are subject to terms, eligibility, and availability. Offers may be modified or canceled at any time with or without notice. See Amazon.com or the Amazon Shopping app for details.
