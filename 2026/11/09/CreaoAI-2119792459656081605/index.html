AI Video Got Easier. Making It Still Feels Like Work.

发表于2026-11-09|行业观察
|浏览量:

AI Video Got Easier. The Workflow Didn’t.

Generating a video is getting ridiculously easy.

Actually getting one you want to post?

Still somehow a process.

Pick a model.

Figure out the prompt.

Render.

Try again.

Realize you made it in the wrong aspect ratio.

Try another model.

Lose the prompt that worked.

Come back three days later and start almost from scratch.

The models got faster. The workflow around them didn’t.

That’s why we built Video Studio in CREAO.

It gives you one place to make, edit, compare, and come back to your videos without rebuilding the process every time.

And if you just want to try it, new accounts start with 30 free credits. No credit card required.

One place for the whole messy part

Video Studio is a dedicated space in CREAO just for making video.

You can start from an example or write your own prompt, watch the render happen there, and come back to every take you’ve made later.

No hunting for the version you liked.

No trying to remember which prompt produced it.

The take and the prompt stay together.

That matters more than it sounds.

Because if you make video regularly for your business, the goal isn’t to create one impressive clip.

It’s to be able to make the next one without rebuilding the process.

You don’t have to start with a blank prompt

Blank prompt boxes are overrated.

Video Studio opens with examples you can actually start from.

Product shots.

Logo reveals.

Interiors.

Cinematic scenes.

Anime.

Character videos.

And more.

Pick one and the prompt and settings are already there.

Render it as-is.

Then change the product.

Change the setting.

Change the style.

Change one line and see what happens.

It’s a much easier way to figure out what works than staring at an empty prompt box trying to remember how many adjectives make something cinematic.

Different ideas need different models

There still isn’t one video model that’s best at everything.

So Video Studio puts multiple options in one place, including:

Veo 3.1

Seedance 2.0

Seedance 2.5

Gemini Omni Flash

That means you can try the same idea with different models instead of deciding upfront which one is supposed to be perfect for the job.

Some are better for cinematic motion.

Some are better for consistency.

Some are better when sound matters.

And sometimes one model just gives you something weird.

Try another.

Choosing the model becomes part of making the video, not a separate research project.

Editing shouldn’t mean starting over

Sometimes a video is almost right.

Which can honestly be more annoying than it being completely wrong.

Maybe the character looks good but the setting doesn’t.

Maybe the product needs to stay the same across several clips.

Maybe you just want to change one thing without regenerating the entire idea.

With Gemini Omni Flash in Video Studio, you can give CREAO an existing video and describe what you want changed.

You can also add reference images to help keep a product, character, or visual style more consistent across clips.

The editing itself is available on the free plan.

Make it once. Post it where it belongs.

A video for TikTok is not necessarily a video for YouTube.

Video Studio supports the formats businesses actually post in.

9:16 for Reels and TikTok.

1:1 for feed posts.

16:9 for YouTube, websites, and product pages.

Plus wider cinematic formats.

You can also describe dialogue, music, or sound effects as part of the prompt and render them with the video.

So the question becomes less:

How do I make an AI video?

and more:

What do I actually want this video to do?

That’s a much more useful question.

The part we think matters most: your takes stay useful

Every take you make stays inside Video Studio with the prompt that created it.

So when something works, you don’t have to treat it like a lucky accident.

Rerun it with one change.

Use the same structure for another product.

Try a different model.

Adapt it for another format.

Come back next week and build from the version that already worked.

For a creator, that means less starting over.

For a small business, it means the product video, ad, or Reel you figured out once can become something you keep improving.

Good work should be reusable.

You probably don’t need another video tool

There are already plenty of those.

What we wanted was a place where the different pieces of AI video creation actually live together:

the examples,

the prompts,

the models,

the editing,

the formats,

the sound,

and the versions you already made.

Less time figuring out the tooling.

More time figuring out what’s actually worth making.

Video Studio is live now in CREAO.

New accounts get 30 free credits with no credit card required, so you can pick an example and see what you make.

Make the first take.
Keep the good ones.
Build from there.

Link to try it in the reply.
