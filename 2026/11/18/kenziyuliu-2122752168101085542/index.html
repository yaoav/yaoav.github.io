A Model of One’s Own

发表于2026-11-18|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @kenziyuliu（Ken Liu） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Introducing Second by First Person Labs PBC

There’s nothing quite like feeling understood.

A sibling who finishes your sentences. An artist who puts your feelings into words. Or a friend who hears “I’m fine” and knows you are not.

The people who know us best are often the ones who can help us the most, because they see beneath the veil of what we say, to what we really mean. And that requires years if not a lifetime of accumulated knowledge and experiences together. They need to build a model of you: not just emails and messages; but who you are, what you do, your people, preferences, goals, commitments, and intent. In other words, they need a whole lot of context.

The same will be true for personal software, as software becomes agents. We believe the next leap in how we interact with machines will be unlocked by an infrastructure that serves full, up-to-the-minute context on us efficiently, securely, and ubiquitously, and that infrastructure is missing.

We now have the tools to make this happen: agents that can watch and learn our every move, and distill our behaviors into re-usable context on an always-on basis. But who will do the watching? Will we be forced to sacrifice our privacy, in exchange? Who will own the model of you that results?

First Person Labs PBC is an applied AI lab founded on the principle that this model of you should belong to you, and it should empower you to do great things.

Your Second and The Model of You

We’re excited to introduce Second, a self-sovereign infrastructure to autonomously build and serve your portable user model across your devices and agents.

Second starts off as a streaming-state, portable context vault that builds itself. Second orchestrates perpetual background agents to continuously learn from your raw computer activity from the OS, up-to-the-minute. There is no action needed from you; your context just comes out. Memories of your recent projects, people, writing samples, or what you did just minutes ago.

Over time, Second builds up a full model of you: your goals, priorities, style, preferences, commitments, and intent. Aspects of you that help you (and your agents) make decisions and drive outcomes. You can then serve Second over both local and cloud APIs or MCPs with your full control—creating “the API of you” for humans and machines alike.

Then, next time you ask, inside Second or elsewhere, there’s no need to explain. Since Second was always with you, it already knows what likely prompted your question and can efficiently serve context around it.

We’ve barely scratched the surface of what portable user models can unlock. Deep understanding of us, served at machine speeds, could drive the next leap in personal software, human-computer interaction, large-scale coordination of humans and agents, and more.

Privacy and Sovereignty are Paramount

In many ways, a model of you is like a home. It holds your most personal things. You decide who comes in, what they see, and when they leave.

Like a home, Second makes it simple to express access controls for your context across your apps, services, and agents. Some visitors get to stay, browse, and do things with you. Others never step inside: they knock, ask for something, and you decide what to share.

Whoever holds our user models will steer what software does for us. We are not here to call out any one company’s agenda or behavior; it’s simply that their priorities and objectives are, naturally, different from yours. For example, narrow user models of our scrolls, swipes, and clicks gave us the feeds we have today, for better and for worse; general user models are far more powerful.

We don’t know yet where this will lead. But we believe putting control into the hands of the people they describe, rather than any single company (including ours), will shape the incentives of the industry for the better.

Holding to this commitment means taking on hard systems and security problems, and making sovereignty an invariant of Second from the ground up. In short:

  • Your model is yours, truly. Second runs on your device by default. It lives in a folder on your computer, where you can inspect it, analyze it, edit it, back it up, or even move it to a USB stick. Maybe someday, you’ll even pass it on to your kids.
  • Bring-your-own intelligence. You can power Second with an existing subscription to Claude or ChatGPT, bring your own API key, use local inference, or buy private inference tokens from us, if you like.
  • We stay off the path. FPL does not see your data. Your data doesn’t pass through FPL, unless you choose our hosted private inference or Second Cloud. We don’t train on, log, or sell your data to anyone (unless you want us to!). If you choose your own hosting and inference provider, your data isn’t with us in the first place.
  • Confidential, from day one. We proudly support zero data retention (ZDR) and offer confidential computing with verifiable controls at every possible opportunity. We host confidential inference, and if you choose to enable Second Cloud, it can run inside a confidential VM.
  • You can run it all. We intend to open-source key components of Second, so you can self-host them right on your laptop, or from the cloud.

As agents come to power all the software we use, your personal context will become ever more precious. With Second, you can create and run your own user model and the API of yourself, and permission access to your agents in a way that feels intuitive.

Who we are

First Person Labs PBC is an applied research and product company studying how AI can understand us deeply, how to protect that understanding, and how we can use it to empower us to do great things.

For decades, researchers have made progress in human-computer interaction, to reduce the latency between what we want to do and how quickly a computer can understand it. Most of these efforts have focused on self-expression: the mouse, the keyboard, the graphical user interface. While we can now type and dictate faster than we ever have, the understanding of us is still missing.

Our vision is for a future where every person is more powerful, because they have the ultimate affordance: personal computers that fully understands them, on their own terms. In this future, there’s no need to explain what you mean or find the right words to explain what you want. Second already knows. It could help you remember what a friend is going through, revisit an idea you abandoned, or see how your priorities have changed over time.

In truth, there’s so much research to do on how we can understand humans, and how we can use that understanding to empower them. We can’t yet imagine all the possibilities.

For us, this vision is only possible when three things come together: empowerment, understanding, and ownership. Empowerment means expanding what people can do, in service of their own goals. Understanding means a detailed model of you, not just connecting data sources. Ownership means this model should be yours to own and control. We think this should define the next era of personal computing.

What now?

Second is available in early access today. We will open for general access soon, as we scale up compute and support. We’re excited to see what amazing things you’ll build on top of your Second.

If the above resonates, we hope you’ll join us on this journey.

Yours, truly.

Ken Liu

Founder, First Person Labs PBC
