Your agent is now a link: introducing Agent Share & Import

发表于2026-11-20|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @MOJOAI_HQ（MOJO） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

You built an agent that works. It watches the price you care about, it acts the way you want, and it keeps a record of every step. Then a friend asks, “how did you set that up?”, and the only way to answer was a screenshot and a long message.

Today that changes. With Agent Share & Import, a MOJO Agent can travel as a single link. Anyone who opens it can read the full plan, have it checked against their own account, and start it themselves. Nothing runs until they press start, and it only ever uses their own money.

Key takeaways

  • Share any agent from its card or its run details. You get a plan poster, a ready-to-paste share text and a link.
  • Hide the amounts with one tap if you’d rather not show your sizing.
  • Whoever opens the link sees the full execution plan before anything else.
  • When a signed-in user opens it, MOJO checks the plan against their account first: available funds, amount, and anything that can’t carry over.
  • If the numbers don’t fit, they can lower the amount or have MOJO draft their own version of the agent.

How to share an agent

  1. Find the agent. Open Assets in the sidebar. In the Agent section, every agent has a share icon next to its status.
  2. Check the card. Tap the share icon and the Share panel opens. On the left is a card for the agent: its name, its result so far (return, principal, how many trade actions it took and its status) and your invite code with a QR code.
  3. Decide on amounts. The Amounts toggle at the top hides the figures, on the card and in the message, so you can share the idea without your sizing.
  4. Send it. Use Save image for the card, Share to social to post it, or Copy message for a ready-to-paste text. The link icon at the top copies just the link. The message reads like this:

At the top of the panel, MOJO says plainly what a share means: anyone who opens it can view the full plan and, once conditions are met, start it with their own account.

The card shows what one agent did in one account over one period. It’s a record, not a promise about the next run.

What happens when someone opens your link

If they aren’t signed in, they land on a public page with the agent’s name, who shared it, what it’s for, a summary and the full execution plan, step by step. Below it, they can sign in or register.

If they’re signed in, MOJO opens the agent straight into its approval page, marked as shared by you, and runs a pre-check against their account:

  • How much they have available on the venue the agent trades on.
  • Whether the amount in the plan fits.
  • Whether any step depends on something that can’t move from your account to theirs.

They can read the summary and the execution plan, edit the agent, or start it. Starting is always their own decision, made with their own funds. Your money, positions and open orders never move.

📷

When the numbers don’t fit

Good plans get shared to people with very different balances. MOJO handles the mismatch instead of leaving the reader to guess:

  • A one-time trade (for example, open one position at market): lower the amount on the approval page. MOJO re-checks it, and if it fits, the agent can start.
  • A repeating or conditional agent (for example, a price check every 10 minutes): you can choose to edit it into your own version. MOJO pre-fills the request, reads the shared plan and your balance, and drafts a new agent sized to what you have. The draft still credits the original sharer.
  • A plan that can’t carry over: some agents rely on things only the sharer has, like a specific open order or existing holdings. MOJO says exactly that, for example: “The plan takes over holdings that already exist in the sharer’s account, so it can’t be started with your account as is.”

Paste links into chat and compare

Share links also work inside a conversation. Paste one into the chat box and MOJO opens it next to your draft, which stays where it was. Paste two, add “compare these two plans”, and MOJO reads both and lines them up for your account. The comparison covers the trigger, the size, the leverage, the worst-case loss, and whether each one can run with what you have right now.

It’s the fastest way to choose between two versions of the same idea, say a USD 2.50 test size and an USD 18 full size, before you start either.
