Thank you

发表于2026-11-20|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @manikgupta（Manik Gupta） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Yesterday, we announced Applied Compute signed an agreement to be acquired by Palo Alto Networks. There’ll be more to share soon, but for now we want to take a beat to thank the people who got us here: the team who built AC, the investors who backed a group of first-time founders, and the customers who trusted a young company with mission-critical work. We really would be nowhere without all of you.

To the Applied Compute team, thank you. Everything this company has achieved in a short time is due to you.

Core Platform built AC2, the product our customers use every day. Ben, our Head of Product, has shaped it since the first design doc. Vinjai and Rahil built much of the early product with him, including the demos that won our first enterprise customers. From there the team made it into something much larger. Aniruddh turned Ari, the assistant inside AC2, into something customers love. Allegra, Felix, and Jason took a tool made for our own researchers and made it one that customers can run themselves and trust to run securely. Camron and Benji keep it close to the people who use it most, our own researchers.

Research Systems built the foundation for the rest of our work. The team is led by Raymond, our Head of Research, who is one of the most talented researchers we’ve ever met. He is a force of nature, incredibly diligent, and a leader whose optimism (and enthusiasm for Snorlax slackmojis) is infectious. Chengyu, Andrew, Chang-Ning are big reasons our training stack is the best in the world for post-training open-weight models. They built much of the ML systems and research that our customers rely on, and they keep it at the frontier by integrating new research as it appears. Edward’s research has deepened our understanding of how models learn from real use. Brian and Aadit have extended our work to new models and made them safer to build on.

Platform Research is where our research becomes product. Sam built the team from scratch with Alex, Jaray, and Nathan. Alex led the research behind our most recent product launch - model routers - which lets customers spend less on AI without giving up quality. Jaray built autoresearch into Ari, so that Ari can run experiments for a customer by itself. Sam has a gift for making hard research clear to the people who need it, and we have yet to put him in front of a customer he did not impress.

Infrastructure keeps all of it running. It is hard to overstate how quickly this team built infrastructure this robust, or how much care they give every customer who runs on it. Margaret, our Infra Lead, has earned the trust of every customer she has worked with. Lucca, Emmanuel, and Michael are the reason new computing capacity works on day 0, remains secure, and our GPUs stay healthy. Bay, Roshan, and Angie serve our customers’ models to their users around the clock with state-of-the-art performance, and treat every customer’s problem as urgent.

Applied Research is the lifeblood of the company. This team works side by side with customers on post-training research for their hardest, most valuable problems. Jacob and Bryan set the standard for how we do that, and taught it to everyone who came after. Daniel, Nic, Pranav, Dylan, Dan, and Evan have carried out some of our largest partnerships. Yi, Ethan, Jason, and Alessandro took ownership of customer work from their first weeks and have quickly ramped up. David runs our most strategic accounts and makes sure they are set up to succeed. And the entire team has contributed back into AC2 so the platform better serves their needs.

Go-to-Market is how the world finds out what we do. Michael, our Chief Business Officer, has been behind many deals we’ve closed. He built our GTM from scratch with Max. Andy, our Head of Sales, now leads the team that brings Applied Compute to the companies that need it: Adam, Ben, Andrew, Courtney, Ryan, and Jacob. Aaron is building our product marketing from the ground up, and has already taken on far more than he was asked to.

Business Operations and Finance built the company around the company. Sahar, our Head of BizOps, is the glue that holds everything together. We can depend on her to fill any gap, and she will always roll up her sleeves for the good of the team. Grant, our Head of Finance, and Wilson built the financial and compute foundation that let us grow. Grant has flexed into whatever role the company needed, and he has brought operational rigor to everything we do. Albert, an engineer by background, is how we find unique and exceptional talent. Amy makes our office a place people want to be, surprising our team with freshly cut fruit, organizing our work trials, and throwing the best Thursday team dinners.

Thank you all for everything you have given this company to set us up for the amazing future ahead.

To our investors, thank you.

We got so lucky with the people who chose to back us. You bet on us early, as young first-time founders, and you have been in our corner ever since.

Elad Gil is one of the clearest thinkers we know. Whenever we faced a hard question about strategy or product, he was the person we could depend on to think it through with us. We always came away seeing the problem more simply.

Victor Lazarte at Diffusion was our first believer. He led our seed round when Applied Compute was three people and an idea, and he has backed us at every step since. As a member of our board, he has helped us steer the business toward the most exciting opportunities, hire our founding team, and close our first customers.

Teddy Citrin at Definition has been an amazing partner on everything to do with customers. He helped us think through how to find them, how to win them, and how to serve them well.

Gaurav Ahuja at Timeless has been a great thought partner. We can bring him a half-formed idea and trust him to take it seriously, push on it, and make it better.

Anton Levy and Myriam Harrouche at Layer Global moved heaven and earth for us. They got us in front of customers we couldn’t have reached on our own, and they helped us recruit the best people.

Brandon Reeves and Lan Jiang at Lux Capital are some of the most loyal partners we have ever known. We’ve known Lan for many years now, and in every moment of crisis, Brandon and Lan were the people we knew we could depend on.

Mike Volpi and Ishani Thakur at Hanabi helped us navigate the compute landscape when capacity was very hard to find. They also helped us recruit top talent, and gave us the kind of seasoned advice very few people can give.

Leigh Marie Braswell at Kleiner Perkins drew on her experience at Scale AI to help us find the bottlenecks in our business and understand how to scale past them. It is rare to have an investor who has already lived the problems you’re facing.

Omri Casspi at Swish is one of the kindest and most generous people we know. He has connected us with customers, opened his remarkable network of CIOs to us, and hosted events with us. Whenever we have asked for help, he has gone out of his way to give it.

Artem Barsukov at NVentures helped us plug into NVIDIA and build a deep relationship there. For a company whose work runs on NVIDIA’s hardware, that relationship has mattered a great deal.

Ali Partovi at Neo believed in all of us early on. He brought us into a community that opened up opportunities and introduced us to people and candidates we would not otherwise have found. All of us are Neo Scholars, and we owe a lot to that community.

Ramtin Naimi and Andrei Kozyrev at Abstract run through walls for us. Whenever we needed an introduction, an answer, or just someone to move fast, they got it done.

Carlo Agostinelli at Nova is an amazing friend and shows more hustle than anyone we have ever met. We can bring Carlo anything and know that he will help.

Ali Rowghani and Amir Movafaghi at First Harmonic have been incredible coaches to us as first-time founders. They helped us grow into the leaders the company needed.

Ilya Kondrashov at Quantum Light Capital connected us to amazing customers, and was generous with every introduction.

Eric Vishria and Peter Fenton at Benchmark bet on us early and then went to work helping us recruit. When we were asking exceptional people to join a young company, it made a difference that Eric and Peter were behind us.

Alfred Lin at Sequoia has been one of the wisest voices we have on strategy and focus. He helped us decide what to do and, just as important, what not to do.

Neil Mehta at Greenoaks connected us to amazing customers and opened doors that would’ve taken us much longer to find and access on our own.

**Lulu Cheng Meservey **is the person we turn to with any question on communications, and a dear friend. She has helped us strategize our positioning and what sets us apart since day one.

Finally, Zach Frankel. Zach taught us what it takes to win. He has been a personal mentor to each of us, and he has consistently pushed us to be the best we could be. He held us to a higher standard than we held ourselves, and he put in the time to help us meet that standard. A great deal of what we got right, we got right because of him.

To our first customers, thank you.

Brendan Foody at Mercor, Scott Wu at Cognition, and Tony Xu and Andy Fang at DoorDash were our first customers. You entrusted real high-stakes problems to a company with no track record, and what we learned working with your world-class engineering teams has been invaluable.

And thank you to every customer who has trusted us since.

To the team at Palo Alto Networks, thank you.

We’re grateful for your confidence in us and excited about our shared vision for the future. We’re looking forward to building with you!

– Yash, Rhythm, and Linden
