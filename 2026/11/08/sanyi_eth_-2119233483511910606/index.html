Mainnet is coming, so it’s time to share more about what you can expect.

We said we’d do things differently. Well, welcome to Season 1.

Trading on Kalos

There’s lots to do on Kalos, and everything counts. Snipe memes. Swap tokens. Provide liquidity. Sweep NFTs.

Each bucket has its own points allocation, and is rewarded individually.

Regardless of how you use Kalos, you’re earning. There is something for everyone.

Revenue Plans

We announced prior that after $KALOS launches, revenue will be distributed on a 90/10 basis - with 90% redirected towards users and 10% towards the protocol. This is true.

During Season 1, it starts at 50/50 to help bootstrap the protocol early on.

Over time, that split gradually shifts toward 90/10, reaching it when $KALOS launches.

Rewards System

The % of revenue set aside for users will be distributed weekly. Real $.

Your Kalos activity directly correlates to what you receive.

Kalos NFT holders will also be eligible for additional rewards through staking.

Refer and Earn

Kalos is better with more traders on it.

Refer a friend. Earn 10% of the fees they generate. Every week.

Points = $KALOS

After Season 1, we launch $KALOS. Your mainnet points will determine your allocation.

Trading on Kalos is the best way to position yourself. Testnet users get a head start, but there are plenty of points to go around.

Get ready.

The Future is Now

If you got this far and only remember two things, using Kalos:

  • earns you real weekly rewards
  • earns you points for the $KALOS airdrop

There is no better place to trade.

Kalos.
