Trade with MOJO, Win an iPhone Duo: MOJO 2026 US Stock Trading Competition

发表于2026-11-16|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @MOJOAI_HQ（MOJO） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Today we are launching the MOJO 2026 US Stock Trading Competition. Applications are open now, and anyone can enter. Trading starts on Mon Nov 23 and runs for 14 days: players will trade real US stocks through MOJO Agent on the exchange they already use, two public leaderboards will rank them live, and people who would rather watch can back a champion from $20 and share the board’s prediction pool.

Three things make this competition different from the ones you have seen on exchanges.

1. MOJO is trading agents that plug straight into your exchange, on your phone. You connect the Binance, OKX, Bybit, Bitget or Hyperliquid account you already have. There is no new venue to fund and no migration. You describe a trade in a sentence, the Agent turns it into a card with the trigger, size, stop and venue, and nothing runs until you approve it. The whole loop fits on a phone screen.

2. Fans put in $20 and share the whole pool. Each board has a champion prediction pool. The champion takes 20% of it, and everyone who backed the champion splits the other 80%. If you registered on MOJO through that trader’s invite code, your share weighs ×1.2. A worked example: a pool ends at $200,000 and only 100 fans backed the champion. The backers split $160,000, which is $1,600 each from a $20 pick, or $1,920 with the invite code. That is an example, not a promise; the real pools start at $2,000 each and grow with every pick.

3. Two leaderboards, a fair fight. One board ranks realized profit in dollars, where size matters. The other ranks return divided by max drawdown, where steadiness matters and a small account can win. Top 3 on both boards take both prizes. Top 10 on either board also get one year of MOJO membership and the MOJO affiliate badge, on top of the cash.

The rest of this post is the full rulebook, written so you can decide in five minutes whether to trade, predict, or both.

Who can enter

Anyone. Applications opened today, Mon Nov 16 (08:00 PT / 16:00 London / Tue Nov 17 00:00 SGT / 01:00 KST). You apply on the competition page: fill in the form, deposit at least 100 USDT into your MOJO account, and sign the rules. Registration complete means you are in. There is no selection round and no limit on the number of players.

Applications stay open until Sun Nov 29 (21:00 PT / Mon Nov 30 05:00 London / 13:00 SGT / 14:00 KST), a week after the opening bell, so you can still join after trading has started.

Every player trades their own funds. Results count capital up to 10,000 USDT; trades made with the part above that are scaled out, so a large account does not buy a place on the board. Withdrawing during the contest counts as quitting. Afterwards, capital and profit can both be withdrawn.

What you can win

The top 10 on either board also get one year of MOJO membership and the MOJO affiliate badge, on top of any cash. A player who finishes in the top 3 of both boards takes both prizes. Cash is paid to your MOJO account after the risk review.

The two boards

Realized PnL ranks profit closed in USD during the contest. This board favours size: $10,100 at +20% is $2,020 of realized profit.

Return ranks return divided by max drawdown, the largest fall from a peak to a later low of your daily-close equity. +20% with a 5% drawdown scores 4.00. +60% with a 40% drawdown scores 1.50, so the steadier account ranks higher despite the smaller gain. The drawdown is floored at 1%, and a max drawdown over 90% takes you off this board.

If you run a concentrated book you are competing for the iPhone Duo on the PnL board. If you trade small and protect capital, the Return board is yours.

What counts

  • US-stock symbols only, on Binance, OKX, Bybit, Bitget or Hyperliquid, through MOJO Agent.
  • 50,000 U of volume to qualify for prizes. Below that you stay on the board but cannot collect a prize.
  • Fair play. Hedging across accounts, self-trading and linked accounts are disqualified. The top 3 on each board are reviewed before payout.

MOJO Agent is how the orders get placed. You describe the trade in a sentence, such as “buy $500 of NVDA if it dips 2% today, stop 5%”, and the Agent turns it into a card with the trigger, size, stop and venue. Nothing runs until you approve it, and you can stop it at any time. Stopping an agent does not close positions.

The champion prediction pool

Each board has a prediction pool. MOJO seeds $2,000 into each, and every pick adds to it.

  • Back one player per board, from $20, deposited to your MOJO account. Top up until picks lock.
  • The champion player takes 20% of their board’s pool.
  • Everyone who backed the champion splits the other 80%, pro rata by amount × weight.
  • Weight is 1.2 if you registered through the champion’s invite link, else 1.0.

A worked example: the pool ends at $200,000 and only 100 fans backed the champion, $20 each. The champion takes $40,000. The 100 backers split $160,000, which is $1,600 each. A backer who registered through the champion’s invite code weighs 1.2 and gets about $1,920. This illustrates the formula; it is not a forecast of any pool’s size.

Picks will open with the boards on Mon Nov 23 and lock on Sun Nov 29 (23:00 PT / Mon Nov 30 07:00 London / 15:00 SGT / 16:00 KST). One account per device and per wallet.

How to start

  1. Apply on the competition page today, deposit at least 100 USDT, sign the rules.
  2. Connect your exchange now, so you are ready to describe your first trade to MOJO Agent at the opening bell on Mon Nov 23.
  3. Share your contest link. Your backers weigh ×1.2 if you win.
