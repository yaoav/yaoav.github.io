Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond

发表于2026-11-16|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @amazon（Amazon） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Denis Villeneuve is directing the Amazon MGM Studios film from a script by Steven Knight, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing and Tanya Lapointe executive producing.

Today, Amazon MGM Studios announced that Robert Pattinson will take on the iconic role of James Bond in the studio’s highly anticipated film, ushering in a new era for one of cinema’s most celebrated characters.

Denis Villeneuve is directing the film from a script by Steven Knight. Amy Pascal and David Heyman are producing through Pascal Pictures and Heyday Films, respectively, with Tanya Lapointe executive producing.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime. I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya, and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team,” said Robert Pattinson.

“Today marks a defining moment for our studio and a significant milestone in the history of James Bond as we proudly introduce Robert Pattinson as the next 007,” said Mike Hopkins, head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios. “This iconic character has captivated audiences around the world for more than six decades, and there’s no doubt that Robert’s remarkable range, magnetic screen presence, and quiet intensity make him an exceptional choice to carry that legacy forward. With Denis Villeneuve at the helm, Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing, Tanya Lapointe executive producing, and a brilliant script from Steven Knight, we have a world-class creative team in place to usher in a new era of Bond, and we couldn’t be more excited about the journey ahead.”

“Few films carry the kind of anticipation that comes with James Bond, and we have strived to honor the passionate fans, the legacy, and the future of this iconic character. We are thrilled to officially welcome Robert Pattinson as our 007 and are confident he will bring something truly special to this role. Robert, Denis, Amy, David, and Tanya are embarking on an amazing adventure together,” said Courtenay Valenti, head of film at Amazon MGM Studios.

“Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin,” said director Denis Villeneuve.

“James Bond is one of the most beloved, inspiring, iconic, and yet enigmatic characters in literature and movies. The legacy of this character is in great hands with Robert Pattinson. His fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit, and his ability to continually reinvent himself make him the perfect Bond for our times,” said producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman.

With a career spanning more than two decades, Pattinson has established himself as one of the most distinctive and versatile actors of his generation, moving seamlessly between independent cinema and major studio productions.

He most recently portrayed journalist Chris Hansen in the psychological thriller Primetime, which had its world premiere at the Venice Film Festival in September. The A24 psychological crime thriller from director Lance Oppenheim marked the first feature film produced by Pattinson’s production company, Icki Eneo Arlo. In July, Pattinson starred as a playfully villainous Antinous in Christopher Nolan’s critically acclaimed blockbuster The Odyssey, with his scene-stealing performance called “endlessly entertaining to watch” and an “outright pantomime.” Earlier this year, Pattinson starred as a happily engaged turned spiraling groom in A24’s darkly funny The Drama, which opened to rave reviews calling Pattinson’s turn as Charlie a “career-best,” “sublime,” and an “exquisite” performance that “proves his dexterity as an actor.”

Later this year, Pattinson will be seen starring as a shapeshifting ‘Face Dancer’ assassin in Dune: Part Three for Denis Villeneuve. Pattinson is also currently in production on Matt Reeves’s The Batman Part II, the sequel to 2022’s highly successful The Batman. Pattinson previously starred in and executive produced Die, My Love, alongside Jennifer Lawrence, and starred in Bong Joon Ho’s critically acclaimed Mickey 17.

Other notable credits include Christopher Nolan’s Tenet, Robert Eggers’ The Lighthouse, and Josh and Benny Safdie’s Good Time. Pattinson first gained industry attention at 19 years of age when he joined the Harry Potter franchise in Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, produced by David Heyman, and is also known worldwide for The Twilight Saga.

In 2022, Amazon acquired MGM, including a vast catalog with more than 4,000 films and 17,000 TV shows. In March 2025, Amazon MGM Studios closed a new joint venture with Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson, the terms of which call for Amazon MGM Studios to gain creative control of the James Bond intellectual property rights.

Robert Pattinson is represented by WME, 3 Arts Entertainment, Curtis Brown Group, Narrative, and Sloane, Offer, Weber & Dern.
