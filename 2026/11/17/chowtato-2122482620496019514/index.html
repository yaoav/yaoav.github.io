My Agentic Conservatorship

发表于2026-11-17|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @chowtato（sarah 😾） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

I’m giving up all financial control to Grok Bot.

@bot has a set budget for Monday to Friday to order my meals, transport me around San Francisco, and schedule activities. It must stay within budget, and re-evaluate my options depending on how much money is left. I will have no purchasing power outside of Grok Bot. Goodbye Chase Sapphire Reserve. Goodbye Apple Pay.

Why would I do this?

To see how far agentic commerce has come, what works well, what is not user friendly, and what hesitations I have while using agents. I’m (wishfully) hoping to find use cases that people outside the SF tech bubble would be interested in.

Grok Bot’s access

Grok Bot was given the following access:

  • My X Money card with instructions to spend up to $250

  • Connections to: DoorDash, Uber, Partiful, Luma, OpenTable, ClipperCard, Gmail, Stripe Link, cafe account, and more

Instructions for the week

  1. Buy me lunch and dinner daily and find creative ways to keep the costs low
  2. Get me to and from work/activities within the budget (walk, Muni, or Uber)
  3. Organize + buy supplies for a get-together with friends
  4. Keep me active/schedule gym time
  5. Plan a date
  6. Update the remaining budget after each day and re-evaluate choices depending on how much is left

Initial musings

  • I’m giving up access to tools that are not accessible by browser. If Grok Bot cannot reach an app to purchase from, I won’t be able to use their services. For example, Lyft and Waymo are out of consideration.
  • I want to be as low touch as possible with Grok Bot. I gave it some of my food preferences, but mostly want it to make decisions without too much back and forth. We know agents can complete a purchase flow - but can it curate products and develop taste?
  • Will Grok Bot stay within budget and can it plan dynamically as the week progresses and budget shrinks?
  • Can Grok Bot make better decisions as it learns more information, or will it try to rely on directions from me?

Each day, I’ll update what Grok Bot decides for me - if I starve or end up walking everywhere in the rain, what the financial damage looks like, and if agents are just this year’s hype cycle or a real game changer. Follow along for a fun experiment to see how far agentic commerce has really come (at my personal detriment).
