Investing in Isomorphic

发表于2026-11-17|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @eriktorenberg（Erik Torenberg） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Drug discovery and development will be one of the largest opportunities for AI. Pharma is already an enormous industry, but there’s still too much disease and too few treatments. The goal is to make better medicines faster and at lower cost, and to treat disease we can’t today.

That is why we’re excited to invest in Isomorphic Labs. Founded by Sir Demis Hassabis, Isomorphic is building an AI-native pharmaceutical company with a mission to solve all disease.

It always starts with the team. Beyond founding and chairing DeepMind and serving as Chief Scientist at Alphabet, Demis (@demishassabis) helped deliver a breakthrough in protein structure prediction that earned him the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. President Max Jaderberg (@maxjaderberg) is a preeminent machine learning researcher and operator. Together, they’ve assembled an exceptional team of AI researchers, engineers, biologists, chemists, and clinicians. Their collective research achievements are remarkable. Their culture is organized around demanding scientific goals and measurable progress.

Isomorphic has brought the pace and ambition of a frontier AI lab to one of the hardest challenges in science, the work of developing new medicines.

Isomorphic’s scientific heritage is AlphaFold, but its work extends well beyond protein structure prediction. The Isomorphic Labs Drug Design Engine (IsoDDE) brings together a suite of models to predict molecular structures and binding, assess drug properties and toxicity, map biological interactions, and design candidate molecules, with reasoning models coordinating the work and helping prioritize which molecules to make and test.

In drug discovery, being faster, cheaper and more effective keeps paying off. In many knowledge work use cases, a model below the frontier might be “good enough” to do the job, so we see diminishing returns to intelligence. But with medicines, we don’t want to settle for “good enough.” Drugs can always get better, and Isomorphic continues to define the frontier.

Isomorphic has emerged as a leading platform, underscored by its partnerships with Eli Lilly, Novartis and Johnson & Johnson as well as its own drug programs. Without giving it all away, here’s one anecdote about Isomorphic’s progress: its AI design agent was able to search and optimize molecular designs over 2-4 days, progressing computationally through iterations that would traditionally require 1-3 months each in the lab. There’s more to come!

We’re particularly excited about how Isomorphic is connecting these efforts as a vertically integrated platform. The ambition extends across the development lifecycle - choosing promising biological targets, optimizing molecules, and ultimately bringing medicines through clinical trials. Better predictions translate to better experiments, which inform the next round of models and designs. This loop compounds as the company takes on more programs.

If Isomorphic can improve the probability of success while reducing development time and cost, more diseases and patient populations become viable to serve. Isomorphic has the potential to be among the most important pharmaceutical companies in the world. The ultimate measure of that progress will be medicines that safely and effectively help patients.

Partnering with Demis, Max and Isomorphic on the mission means a lot to all of us at a16z.

For me, this is personal. My family has lost loved ones to heart disease and leukemia too soon, and every family has stories like this. When I look at my two children, I hope their generation will have a very different experience of health and aging. I’d love for them to live healthy lives well past 100. Isomorphic makes us optimistic about a healthier future.

More healthy years with the people you love. It’s hard to think of a more important application of AI.

Thanks to @justin_kahl and @CarolineGoggs for coauthoring this with me.
