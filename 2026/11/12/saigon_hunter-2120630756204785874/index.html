Every coin on Elysium gets a perp engine.

发表于2026-11-12|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @saigon_hunter（Saigon Hunter） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Perpium is a layer built on top of launchpads, starting with Ascend on Elysium.

Every coin launched through Perpium gets its own live Hyperliquid position, paid for by its own trading fees. From the very first trade, part of every fee is set aside for the engine.

And nothing about the launchpad changes. A coin launched through Perpium is still a normal launchpad coin, with the same curve and the same trading. The difference is that part of its fees is routed into a live perpetual futures position on Hyperliquid, held in an account that belongs to that coin alone.

We call that position the coin’s perp engine.

A perp engine is a real position on Hyperliquid, funded only by a coin’s trading fees, owned by the coin’s own account, and run by rules instead of a person.

The stack, read like a periodic table

At the base is the chain: Elysium, built by Kinetiq. It’s where coins live, fees land and every split is recorded.

One row up are the launchpads. Ascend is the first. It provides the curve, the trading and the 1% fee that powers everything above it.

Above the launchpads sits Perpium, the engine layer. It doesn’t replace the launchpad or fork its curve. It takes the fees the coin has already earned and gives them a job.

From the top, the engine reaches out to markets: @Hyperliquid crypto perps, and through HIP-3 and @Markets_xyz, stocks, commodities and FX. More than 300 markets in all.

The coin is still an launchpad coin

A Perpium coin isn’t a rival to a launchpad coin. It is a launchpad coin. For example It launches on Ascend, trades on Ascend’s curve, and pays Ascend’s 1% fee on every trade, exactly like its neighbours.

Perpium operates as an overlay. Each coin’s fee allocation settles into its own dedicated wallet on Elysium, where the engine routes and apportions it on chain according to predefined parameters the creator sets at launch. The bonding curve is never touched.

In fact, the launchpad gains. A coin with an engine gives people more reasons to trade it and hold it, and every one of those trades pays the launchpad’s fee. Perpium adds to every party it touches.

Meet TTA (TestTokenA), our worked example

Imagine a meme coin called TestTokenA, launched through Perpium on Ascend. TestTokenA is made up, and every number attached to it is an illustration, not a forecast.

Say TestTokenA trades $100,000 in a day. Ascend’s 1% fee on that is $1,000. Assume $400 of it reaches TestTokenA’s wallet. Everything else follows from that $400.

Step one: the split

Creators choose the split based on what the project needs; it’s up to them to find the best strategy. For example The engine can allocate the $400 across six buckets:

  • Engine, 40%, becomes the perp position
  • Holders, 20%
  • Creator, 10%
  • Buyback, 15%
  • Stakers, 10%, paid through the weekly farm
  • Perpium treasury, 5% - Acquires $KNTQ & $HYPE

Perpium’s 5% is split down the middle. Half goes into a treasury that accumulates $HYPE and $KNTQ, the tokens of the two ecosystems Perpium is built on. The other half funds Perpium.

Creators can pick other presets, with one hard floor: the engine never receives less than 25%.

Step two: the engine

The engine’s capital doesn’t idle. In the coin’s base asset (USDC, HYPE or KNTQ, matching its pair), it is bridged to Hyperliquid within seconds via Relay, into an account that belongs to TestTokenA and nothing else.

Each coin’s Hyperliquid account is its own public address. No shared fund. To see what TestTokenA’s engine holds, you look up the address yourself.

The creator picks the market at launch: a crypto perp, a stock, a commodity or a currency through HIP-3, or a basket of markets run as one engine.

From there, the engine runs autonomously. It computes each incoming allocation, sizes the position, and opens or tops it up as new fees arrive. Risk is governed by a predefined rule set: native stop orders on the venue, deleveraging thresholds, and pause controls. **No discretionary trading. Every action is rules-based.
**

Step three: the harvest

Say TestTokenA’s engine was built from $4,000 of fees and is now worth $6,000, up 50%. The engine closes a quarter of the position, realising $500 of profit, split like this:

  • Holders, 35% ($175)
  • Back into the engine, 25% ($125)
  • Stakers, 20% ($100)
  • Perpium treasury, 20% ($100)

The other three quarters stays open and keeps running. If the engine lost money before, new profit repays those losses first.

What if the engine loses?

We have measures in place to make sure positions don’t go underwater, but engine losses can still happen. Same coin, bad week: the engine falls from $6,000 to $4,000, a real $2,000 loss.

What didn’t happen: holders’ coins weren’t touched. Stakers’ principal wasn’t touched. The loss stopped at the engine. It was built from fees, and fees are all it can lose.

Then the refill starts. With fresh fees rolling in, it fills up again.

A team can fund its runway without selling

Teams usually pay for building by selling their own tokens. Perpium offers another way: the project treasury. A team can send up to 20% of the engine’s share to a project address for the first 12 weeks, then up to 5% for good.

A team version of TestTokenA at the full 20%: from the engine, portion of the engine share goes to the project and the rest still feeds the engine. Over 12 weeks that’s about amounts a decent size for a runway, with zero tokens sold.

Some safeguards: the treasury address is shown on the coin page, and **the slice can only be lowered, never raised. **The next phase will add Safe Multisig treasuries.

What’s in the app

1- Studio is where coins are launched. Pick the pair (USDC, HYPE or KNTQ), the engine’s market and the fee preset, and the coin goes live on Ascend with its engine attached.

2-Earn holds the weekly staking farm.

Stake a coin and earn its share of fees and engine profit every week. This mechanism rewards users, reduces selling pressure and increases buying pressure for the token. The Farms use a **ve(3,3) **style decaying lock. Your reward power depends on how much you stake and how long your lock has left, up to 30 days. Power decays a little each day as the unlock date gets closer, so the longest lock earns the most. Relock at any time to bring your power back to full.

3- Games

Earn, with one rule: you play with yield, never with your coins. Reaction is the weekly duel between two coins’ engines. Call the Charge lets stakers call their engine up or down. The Isotope draw gives one ticket per 100 coins held or staked, and nobody pays to enter.

4- The Arena

Is the leaderboard: every coin ranked by engine PnL. Every engine is a public Hyperliquid account, so anyone can check it.

Perpium is Win-Win for the entire ecosystem

The FlyWheel Effect:

Launchpads gave every idea a coin. Perpium gives every coin an engine.
