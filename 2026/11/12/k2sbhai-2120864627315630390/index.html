How to turn Grok Bot into your app's social media manager (Full Setup)

发表于2026-11-12|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @k2sbhai（K2S） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

X just put out a full guide telling creators to let Grok Bot handle all their busywork.

But I think founders are about to use it wrong and miss out on 90% of what it’s capable of doing for you and your business.

(FYI I’m giving away all 7 prompts in this article AND the full setup doc I’d paste into my bot on day one, all you need to do is comment “BOT” and follow me so I can send it to you).

**Here’s what I mean: **

Making content for marketing is basically free now.

AI UGC, slideshows, animated reels, all of it costs pennies and takes minutes.

The tricky part is now everything that happens after you hit post.

  • A post goes viral and there’s no link anywhere near it
  • A winner gets posted once on one platform and never again
  • Your content keeps flopping because nobody looked at the numbers

Every post that pops without a way to find your app is giving those downloads away.

So this setup does two things:

Grok Bot finds what’s going viral on X, TikTok, and Instagram today and turns it into posts about your app.

Postiz gets those posts onto every platform, then runs the automations that keep a viral post earning long after you’ve moved on.

Here’s everything I’m going to walk you through:

  • Why Grok Bot, and why right now
  • Building a bot that actually knows your app
  • Connecting Postiz (about 2 minutes)
  • The daily trend loop
  • Scheduling a week in one message
  • The 5 automations that plug the leaks
  • The Monday read
  • Where you still need a human

All you need is a Grok Bot, a Postiz account and a free Apify account.

Let’s get into it.

1. Why Grok Bot, and why right now

Three reasons:

First, it lives on X and has direct access to the X API.

Every other agent has to go looking for what’s trending.

Grok Bot is sitting inside the place where trends start, so the trend scan is the easiest part of this whole system.

Second, it has its own cloud computer and runs routines on a schedule, even when your laptop is closed.

You set a job once (scan trends every morning, check analytics every hour, write the Monday report) and it keeps running while you sleep.

Third, X is pushing it hard.

Their creator guide did 481K views in four days.

When a platform is promoting a new feature, the people who build on it early get the attention.

Grok Bot can post for you out of the box.

What Postiz adds is the system around the posting: a real calendar, drafts you approve, 30+ platforms from one place, analytics your bots can read, and automations that fire after the post goes live.

2. Build a bot that actually knows your app

Your bot is only as good as the context you give it.

X’s own guide says the same thing, and they’re right.

So use voice dictation and just brain dump everything about your app and build an app brain.

What your app does, who it’s for, the one moment where a user goes “oh, that’s cool,” what your users complain about, and your 10 best-performing posts ever.

PROMPT 1: Build the app brain

‼️ If you want the full Grok Bot setup doc, just comment “BOT” and I’ll send it right over ‼️

In Grok Bot, open the Connected apps section and search for Postiz.

Click add, then sign in to your **Postiz **account when it asks.

Then ask it to list your connected accounts.

If it comes back with your channels, you’re live.

Before you let it touch anything, give it house rules.

This is the most important prompt in the article.

PROMPT 2: House rules

4. The daily trend loop

Every morning, the bot looks at what’s moving on X, TikTok, and Instagram and asks one question: is there an honest way to connect this to my app?

Most trends won’t pass, and that’s fine.

A forced trend tie-in is worse than no post at all.

X is the easy part, because Grok Bot can search it on its own.

TikTok and Instagram are where most consumer apps actually grow, and the bot can’t see either one without help.

That’s what the Apify plugin is for.

In Grok Bot, open Settings, then Plugins, and search for Apify.

Click add and sign in to your Apify account (the free plan is enough to test this).

Then type @apify in any chat, and the bot can run TikTok and Instagram scrapers for you.

Have it rank what it finds by save rate.

People save the posts they want to come back to, so a save tells you more than a like.

Apify charges per result, so start with 5 hashtags or search terms and add more once you see the bill.

Once you pick the trends worth riding, the bot writes the set.

One idea, adapted to every platform you’re on.

PROMPT 4: Write the set

5. Schedule the week in one message

Once you’ve approved the drafts, you don’t need to drag anything around a calendar.

Tell the bot when your audience is most active and let it fill the slots.

PROMPT 5: Fill the week

6. The 5 automations that plug the leaks

These are the most common leaks in your marketing strategy that this setup can address:

Leak 1: Nobody can find your app.

Every post gets an automatic first comment with your link.

I do this on every LinkedIn post I publish.

Postiz drops a comment pointing to my newsletter 15 minutes after the post goes live, and I never type it by hand.

For your app, the comment is one line and the App Store link.

Leak 2: Your winner only lives on one platform.

A post that pops on X has already passed the hardest test there is.

Within 24 hours, it should be on every other platform you’re on, adapted for each one and Postiz helps you make that happen in less time with the AI agent you have at your disposal.

Leak 3: You only post your winners once.

Your followers saw it.

The other 99% of the platform didn’t.

Set your best evergreen posts to repeat every 30 days and let them keep working.

Leak 4: Your other accounts are sitting idle.

If you have a founder account and a brand account (you should), one should repost the other’s best work automatically.

Leak 5: Nobody’s watching when something takes off.

This is the one I’d set up first…

PROMPT 6: Viral alert (set as an hourly routine)

When a post takes off, the first few hours decide how much you get out of it.

This routine makes sure you’re there for them.

7. The Monday read

The true scoreboard is downloads, views are nice but only if they drive conversions.

A post that does 400K views and sends you 40 downloads is worse than one that does 60K and sends you 60.

Every Monday, the bot sorts last week’s posts into four buckets.

PROMPT 7: Monday report (set as a weekly routine)

The failure log is the most valuable file in the whole system.

Every flop becomes more data to iterate off of, and the bot takes that info into consideration before your next batch of content.

If you’re getting downloads but nobody’s paying, stop posting.

That’s an onboarding or paywall problem, and no amount of content will fix it.

8. Where you still need a human

You can hand most of this to the bot

These parts stay yours:

  • The app brief. Nobody knows your magic moment better than you.
  • Approving trends. The bot will happily tie your app to something it shouldn’t. You’re the brand safety check.
  • Approving every batch. Nothing goes live without a yes from you.
  • Replying to comments. Real replies to real people.
  • The kill and scale calls. The bot sorts the buckets, you make the decision.

Grok Bot makes the content machine easy to start, and it’s still up to you to do the unsexy simple things longer and faster than everyone else.
