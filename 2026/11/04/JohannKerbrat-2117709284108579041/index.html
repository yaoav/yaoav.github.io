What's on Robinhood Chain? Ecosystem launches, milestones, and new builder grants

发表于2026-11-04|行业观察
|浏览量:

Welcome to our new roundup of what’s happening on Robinhood Chain: who’s building, what’s shipping, and what’s getting tokenized.

Shout out to everyone supporting our vision to bring more assets onchain and into the apps people use, on infrastructure purpose-built to support them. 💚

Under the hood 🚄

🔹Top protocols by TVL: @Morpho $582.17M, @Uniswap $280.9M, @Lighter_xyz $112.02M, @arcus_xyz $63.61M, @uponrh $4.52M
🔹Robinhood Chain TVL: $1.07B
🔹Cumulative DEX volume: $120.46B
🔹 125 days post-launch, Robinhood Chain became the fastest chain ever to reach $100B in volume on @Uniswap

Builder boost 🔊

Together with @Arbitrum, we’re supporting builders across the Robinhood Chain ecosystem through Open House, an ongoing series focused on developers, founders, and mentorship, with $1.8M in total grants to support projects that launch on the chain.

Next up: Cohort 2 of the Arbitrum Mentorship Program, a 12-week program that includes opportunities to:

🔹Connect with investors and ecosystem partners.
🔹Pitch your project at Demo Day.
🔹**Win $100K in grants. **

Applications are open until December 6, 2026. Apply here.

Builder zone 🏗️

@Morpho: Brought Robinhood Stock Tokens to its credit markets, so eligible users can borrow stablecoins against them as collateral and any app can integrate them.

@aeroxyz: Launched its new multichain DEX, integrating Robinhood Chain.

@ClutchMarkets & @OxSimpleFarmer: Launched a new experiment on Robinhood Chain: Pair Tokens, a single ERC-20 token that wraps a full range Uniswap v4 liquidity position for two assets, with mint and redeem available in one or both.

@metricxyz: Launched its DEX on Robinhood Chain, with Active Pools and propAMM infrastructure built for tokenized RWAs.

@priors_trade: Launched a new AI agent record board on Robinhood Chain.

@SushiSwap: Enabled multi-asset trading on Robinhood Chain through its partnership with @LayerZero_Core.

@RamsesExchange: Integrated markets and liquidity for Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain, with $2.9B+ in trading volume and 30+ Stock Tokens.

@glider__: Launched infrastructure for users to build Stock Token indexes on Robinhood Chain.

@netnetcap: Released Phase 1 of Net Forge, an open-source game engine on Robinhood Chain that gives developers ready-made games to customize and build on.

@pendle_fi: Launched fixed yield, leveraged yield exposure, and liquidity provision on Robinhood Chain.

@colosseum: Ran a dedicated Robinhood Chain hackathon track at the Crypto World’s Fair, with $25K for the top 5 startups. Winners to be announced soon.

@arcus_xyz: Launched liquidity and execution infrastructure for Stock Token spot trading in Robinhood Wallet, with 200+ Stock Tokens.

@KyberNetwork: Launched KyberSwap trading on Robinhood Chain.

@Mercuryo_io: Launched on-ramps for ETH and USDG on Robinhood Chain.

@nansen_ai: Integrated its data tracking system with Robinhood Chain, covering network activity, transaction flows, and ecosystem growth.

@IndexifyApp: Added Robinhood Chain assets to its onchain index platform.

@DatalineAI: Connected its API layer to Robinhood Chain, covering price, funding, positions, and prediction markets.

@CatalystMkts_: Launched event-driven markets tied to real-world events on Robinhood Chain.

@uponrh: Launched a native exchange and liquidity layer for Robinhood Chain.

@ZenGo: Integrated Stock Tokens and crypto on Robinhood Chain in one self-custodial wallet.

@NEARProtocol: Launched swaps, deposits, and withdrawals to and from Robinhood Chain across 30+ chains.

@Moshdottrade: Released infrastructure for 96 AI agents that manage trading for assets on Robinhood Chain.
