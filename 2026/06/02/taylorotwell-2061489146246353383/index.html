It's never been a better time to build

发表于2026-06-02|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @taylorotwell（Taylor Otwell） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

I built Laravel because I wanted to bring my own ideas to life faster. I had projects I wanted to ship, and the existing options slowed me down.

Every feature, package, and product I’ve built since is chasing that same goal: remove the friction between an idea and production. Laravel Forge made it easy to provision and manage servers. Laravel Cloud takes that a step further: get your app online in one minute or less, so you can start serving real customers and build a business.

But, as I chatted with the community, two things kept coming up that prevented them from committing to Cloud: uncertainty about the monthly bill, and hibernation wake times that made it impractical for user-facing apps. At 8-10 seconds, you couldn’t really leave hibernation on for anything users might hit — but keeping your app up 24/7 on Cloud significantly more expensive than managing a VPS yourself on Forge.

Together, they gave developers the sense that Cloud was for ‘real businesses’ only — not the hobby projects and side hustles that are the lifeblood of our community and where every next big thing starts.

So, we’re fixing that.

$5/Month, No Surprises

Starting today, Cloud’s Starter plan is $5/month with $5 in monthly usage credits included. If you’re a new signup, your first month is free.

We’re also launching spend caps. Set a hard monthly spending limit, get alerts at 50%, 75%, and 90%, and we’ll shut off compute when you hit the ceiling unless you tell us to keep it running. You define your maximum spend before the month starts.

Scale Your Entire Stack to Zero

Our next-generation scale-to-zero compute replaces Cloud’s old hibernation system. It’s the key to running every idea you have on a single $5 plan.

Hibernation has been part of Cloud for a while, but it was slow. Wake times were around 10 seconds, and not all of your infrastructure would scale down alongside compute.

We thought that was unacceptable, so we figured out how to wake Kubernetes pods in under 500ms. Essentially a blink. 20x faster than before.

The full stack — compute, databases, cache, and queues — now scales to zero and wakes in milliseconds. And, even when scaled to zero, your app now wakes to run scheduled tasks and process incoming jobs, then scales right back down. Your users won’t notice the difference, but your bill will.

Multiple side projects can now live on Cloud for $5/month and you’ll barely touch that budget.

What Else Has Shipped Recently

We’ve been shipping other stuff too. In the last few months, we shipped:

  • Autoscaling queue workers via managed queues
  • Basic RBAC across all plans, advanced custom roles and granular scopes on higher tiers
  • HTTP basic auth for staging and preview environments
  • Scheduled autoscaling: create recurring schedules to adjust replica counts ahead of predictable traffic spikes
  • Redesigned usage page: to-the-hour breakdown by app and resource
  • Full mobile support

The best part of building Laravel has always been the community building alongside us. I hope these changes make it easier to turn an idea into something real — to spin up the side project, ship the prototype, and find out if that weekend experiment is actually the next big thing. Thank you for the feedback, the ideas, and the energy that keeps us going. 🫶
