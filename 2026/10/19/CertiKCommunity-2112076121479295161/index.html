PopDEX Open Beta Is Live: The Trader-First Perp DEX

发表于2026-10-19|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @CertiKCommunity（CertiK Skynet） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Built for real trading: execution, transparency, and value that work for the traders who make the market.

An exchange ultimately earns its place through every trade it executes. For traders, competitive fees are only part of the equation. What matters is the price they actually get, whether execution remains reliable during volatile markets, how efficiently their capital can be deployed, and whether they can verify what actually happened. These are the fundamentals of trading. Rewards matter too, but they should be built on these fundamentals, not used to replace them.

PopDEX starts with the trader. Trader-first shows up in two places: how the product is built, and how trading is rewarded.

**In product design, **every feature answers to those fundamentals: low fees and deep liquidity, so traders get better prices even on size; a fully on-chain order book, so every trade can be verified; high-performance execution, so orders hold up when markets move fast; and a unified account across crypto, equities, and commodities, so capital works across markets instead of sitting idle.

In incentive design, PopDEX returns value to the people who create it, not to token holders who don’t trade. Through our 100% Value Return framework, all distributable value from trading fees flows back to traders and ecosystem contributors. That includes MegaPop, a weekly USDT prize pool for real traders, with every draw verifiable on Bitcoin.

PopDEX enters Open Beta with approximately $70M in TVL, $4.5B in cumulative Closed Beta trading volume, and 66 markets.

Fully On-Chain. Built for Speed.

Decentralization should go beyond self-custody. On many DEXs, order books and matching engines run off-chain, while only final settlement reaches the blockchain. Order processing, cancellations, and matching remain in the venue’s own records. Traders can verify the outcome, but not necessarily the process.

Every order on-chain. Every fill verifiable. Built on Morph Tachyon, a purpose-built high-performance trading Layer 1 developed by the Morph team, PopDEX operates a fully on-chain Central Limit Order Book (CLOB). Order submission, cancellation, matching, and settlement all happen on-chain. Every order, fill, and reward distribution leaves a traceable record that traders can independently inspect through the PopDEX Block Explorer. Whether you’re reviewing an execution or tracing activity during volatile markets, the underlying record is there.

Built for speed. No trade-off on transparency. Morph Tachyon targets 100ms block times, up to 200,000 TPS, and instant finality. Combined with PopDEX’s sub-second execution, this architecture brings professional trading performance and on-chain verifiability into the same system.

Traders should never have to choose between execution performance and on-chain transparency.

Competitive Costs. Reliable Execution.

For an active trader, the cost of a trade is never just the fee on a pricing page. It is the fee, the depth available when an order reaches the book, and the slippage incurred during execution. A low fee means little if the order hits a thin book.

Lower fees through trading. Zero additional liquidation fees. PopDEX offers trading fees nearly 30% lower than leading Perp DEXs, with VIP volume thresholds approximately 40% lower than comparable platforms. Active traders can unlock and keep lower fees through trading alone, without buying or staking a platform token. Positions liquidated through the order book incur standard trading fees only, with no additional liquidation surcharge.

Deep liquidity. Built for real execution. Liquidity has been central to PopDEX’s market design from day one. Transaction sequencing, fee structures, and rate limits on the trading L1 are designed to support continuous quoting and meaningful order book depth, enabling competitive execution costs even for large orders.

These are the costs traders feel in every fill. That is where PopDEX competes.

One Account. More Markets. Greater Capital Efficiency.

Trading opportunities don’t stay within a single market. Yet capital is often fragmented across accounts, assets, and venues. Moving funds, managing separate collateral balances, and switching between platforms add friction when traders need to act quickly.

PopDEX brings crypto and RWA perpetuals together under a unified trading account, allowing traders to manage positions across supported markets in one trading environment. Whether the opportunity is in crypto, equities, or commodities, traders can follow the markets that matter without constantly switching between separate trading systems.

Capital access is part of that experience. PopDEX supports USDT deposits and withdrawals through Arbitrum, connecting traders from one of DeFi’s most established ecosystems to PopDEX’s crypto and RWA perpetual markets. Users can fund their PopDEX accounts directly from Arbitrum, making it easier to put existing on-chain capital to work.

USDT deposits and withdrawals are also supported through Morph, with USDC support and additional networks planned.

The unified account is also the foundation for greater capital efficiency ahead. Planned developments include spot trading, multi-collateral support that allows more assets to serve as margin, and new ways for idle capital to stay productive, so less of it sits unused.

Our long-term goal is simple: traders should spend less time managing fragmented accounts and more time acting on market opportunities.

Risk Controls Built for Volatile Markets

The differences between trading venues are not always obvious when markets are calm. The real test comes when prices move sharply, liquidity shifts quickly, and traders need reliable execution the most.

Protection against abnormal prices. PopDEX integrates risk controls into its protocol design. Its oracle, index-price, and mark-price systems include safeguards** designed to **reduce the impact of unreliable or dislocated price inputs on position valuations and liquidation decisions. A short-lived price anomaly alone should not be enough to trigger liquidation.

These safeguards mitigate unintended risks, not eliminate market risk or guarantee protection against liquidation. As PopDEX expands into more markets, its pricing protections and risk management mechanisms will continue to evolve.

Transparent risk management, verifiable on-chain. Through the PopDEX Block Explorer, traders can inspect not only orders and fills, but also risk management actions, including liquidations and auto-deleveraging (ADL). These on-chain records allow users to independently review how the protocol responds during volatile markets, rather than relying solely on the platform’s own reporting.

Risk management should not be a black box. At PopDEX, transparency extends from trade execution to the actions that protect the market.

MegaPop: Real Trading. Real Rewards.

On many Perp DEXs, trading fees fund token buybacks. That rewards token holders, not necessarily the traders who paid the fees. MegaPop takes a different approach. It is PopDEX’s fully on-chain, automated weekly rewards system, built to reward real trading activity rather than token ownership or promises of future airdrops.

Trade to earn. Draw every week. Eligible trading activity builds progress toward Pop Tickets. Once issued, each Ticket automatically enters the weekly Pop Draw. The USDT prize pool grows with trading activity, featuring multiple prize tiers, including the Mega Prize and Super Prize. Every valid Ticket carries a chance to win big.

Funded by fees. Verified by Bitcoin. MegaPop is part of PopDEX’s 100% Value Return framework: all distributable value from trading fees flows back to traders and ecosystem contributors. 40% funds the weekly MegaPop pool, and 60% funds ecosystem incentives, from trading competitions to referral and affiliate programs.

From Ticket issuance to weekly draws and reward distributions, the entire process is automated on-chain. Each Pop Draw uses a predetermined Bitcoin block hash to generate a six-digit Pop Hash, matched against valid Pop Tickets. All records are publicly verifiable.

More Tickets mean more chances to win, but trading volume alone does not decide the prize. Traders of different sizes have a shot at the top tiers.

This is what “Trade. Pop. Repeat.” stands for: trade to earn Tickets, experience the excitement of a weekly draw, and return for the next cycle of trading and rewards.

PopDEX Open Beta is now live. Trade now in your browser on desktop or mobile, or download the Android app on Google Play. iOS is coming soon.
