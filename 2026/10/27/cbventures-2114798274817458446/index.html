Mochatrade receives $500k in USDC from YC

发表于2026-10-27|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @cbventures（Coinbase Ventures 🛡️） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

On 16 October 2026, Mochatrade received $1 from @ycombinator.

It arrived in USDC on Base. A test transaction, to check that the receiving wallet address worked.

The $1 was followed by $124,999, then $375,000. Each transfer arrived in seconds. Our full $500K YC investment, sent as stablecoins instead of a wire.

All of this happened on a Friday evening when all the bank wires were closed.

For startups, the promise of stablecoins is straightforward: faster, cheaper payments that work globally, making money as easy to send as a text message. Receiving our YC investment this way made that tangible for us.

YC sees this extending across startup finance. As more companies are built in the age of AI, it expects much of their financial infrastructure to move onchain, from everyday payments to raising capital.

For a team building for India, there was something familiar about that.

In India, everyday money already moves this fast through UPI. You pay for tea by scanning a QR code, and the shopkeeper’s speaker announces the payment before you put your phone away.

Business banking has been slower to catch up. A seed check still usually travels by wire: banking hours, transaction fees, cut-off times, and a bank or two in the middle. Receiving our investment this way felt like startup funding catching up with an experience we already take for granted.

Mochatrade is built the same way.

Indian traders trade Nifty options in rupees. They want to trade US markets with the same ease, but many of the existing routes were built for long-term investors. On Mochatrade, they can trade perpetual futures linked to NVDA, TSLA and other US stocks. Mochatrade users deposit rupees through UPI and withdraw rupees to their bank.

Our YC investment brought together three pieces of infrastructure that made the experience straightforward:

**@base **was the network the investment travelled on, with network fees of a fraction of a cent.
Note: Coinbase Ventures is also an investor on our cap-table.

**@circle **issues USDC, the dollar-backed stablecoin we received. It is backed by cash and cash-equivalent assets and is natively available on Base.

The funds now sit in our @tryramp Stablecoin Account, where we can send onchain payments and fund everyday business bills. That matters to us as a startup: the money arrived quickly, and it’s already in the account we use to run the business.

When YC announced this option in February, it pointed to India as one of the places where stablecoins already power faster, cheaper financial services

For us, this was a small glimpse of how startup funding could feel for global teams: as ordinary as paying for tea.

We sat down with @nemild for a 10-minute conversation about it, shared alongside this article.

We have over 25,000 people on the waitlist and have started sending early access invites. Join the waitlist at mochatrade.com/waitlist
