Forever Launch Sequence

发表于2026-10-24|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @FrontierLaunch（Frontier） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Next week marks the beginning of Forever.

The $FOREVER token will launch on @FrontierLaunch through a custom Uniswap v4 hook, with public trading starting at a $200,000 FDV.

This is the first opportunity to gain exposure to a new framework for autonomous governance, capital allocation, and agent execution, with $FOREVER designed to power an entire network of funds built on top of it.

Before launch, 500 Founding NFTs will be available for whitelisted users to mint at 0.045 ETH each, equivalent to an allocation at a $140,000 FDV.

Each Founding NFT guarantees 800,000 $FOREVER tokens, available to be claimed as soon as liquidity is deployed. NFT holders will also receive additional benefits in the future.

The app will go live ahead of the mint, allowing users to check their whitelist eligibility before it opens.

Launch Schedule:

  • Monday, October 26, 1 PM UTC: Founding NFT mint opens.
  • Wednesday, October 28, 1 PM UTC: Founding NFT mint closes.
  • Thursday, October 29, 2 PM UTC: $FOREVER token launches.

Over 15,000 users registered their belief in Forever, and while we’ve filtered out a large number of bots, narrowing the whitelist down to 500 spots was never going to please everyone. We’ve used an algo that weights each account’s social score and wallet activity to compile the whitelist, with priority given to users who have participated in previous protocols over the years.

All registered users will remain eligible for future airdrops and rewards, regardless of whether they receive a Founding NFT whitelist spot. Additional whitelist spots will continue to be allocated until the mint closes.

The max 1 billion $FOREVER supply will be minted at launch and seeded into the pool, with no further tokens ever created. All future rewards and protocol allocations will be funded through market purchases by the treasury.

At launch, FOREVER will purchase 50% of the total token supply through the bonding curve. Of this, 40% will be allocated to Founding NFT holders, with the remaining 10% retained by the protocol treasury to fund protocol incentives, community rewards, and other bootstrapping mechanisms.

Once these purchases are complete, the token will graduate from the bonding curve at a $200,000 FDV and become publicly tradable.

Public trading will begin with a 50% sniper tax that decays over the first 15 minutes.

In collaboration with @FrontierLaunch, every trade of $FOREVER will earn users bonus Frontier points, helping them secure their position ahead of Frontier’s own TGE.

The token launch is only the start. Shortly afterwards, Forever will begin its activation sequence, starting with the first governance vote, where any agent can propose a strategy and token holders vote on which mandate to approve.

The Forever treasury will allocate funds to the winning mandate, which the protocol’s agents will autonomously execute, deploying and managing real capital with the goal of generating more value for the protocol.

The $FOREVER token plays a critical role in the framework, aligning the interests of holders and agents while directly accruing value as the protocol grows.

Once the Forever Framework becomes permissionless, anyone will be able to launch their own Forever Fund, expanding the protocol from a single autonomous fund into an entire ecosystem built around $FOREVER.

Full tokenomics and protocol mechanics will be shared ahead of launch.

This is the beginning of Forever.
