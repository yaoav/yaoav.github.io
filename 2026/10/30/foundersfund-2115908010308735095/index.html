Accepted

发表于2026-10-30|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @foundersfund（Founders Fund） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission has accepted General Matter’s first license application for review. Acceptance marks the beginning of our formal license application review, a major milestone on our mission of fueling American power.

In its acceptance letter, the NRC noted “the quality and completeness of the application reflect the effectiveness of General Matter’s constructive pre-application engagement with the NRC staff,” enabling the NRC “to accept the application in approximately half the timeframe typically allotted for an acceptance review.” The NRC also said it “will pursue appropriate efficiencies and consider opportunities to expedite portions of the review.”

As we shared last month, following this first license application we intend to submit a license amendment to produce High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium (HALEU) next year.

This two-part approach — a core application followed by an amendment for higher assays — reflects many months of open communication with the NRC. We believe it will lead to faster licensing and authorization, bringing safe, secure, and commercially competitive American enrichment online sooner. Beginning at lower assays will allow us to log operating hours and demonstrate safe, reliable performance ahead of production at higher assays.

Since our filing four weeks ago, the NRC has begun publishing portions of our application publicly, including our 457-page environmental report. We will continue our ongoing meetings with neighbors and community members, sharing relevant information whenever possible. As we shared last month, we’ve requested to keep some parts of our application confidential, a standard industry practice to protect proprietary and security-related information.

Our team now stands at over 200 full-time employees, with centuries of combined nuclear experience embedded in our work. Our intense focus on talent has created a workforce comprised of the highest performers from America’s nuclear industry and the world’s most innovative technology companies.

As we said at the time of our filing, the urgency of this moment demands we act now and move with efficiency.

We will enrich uranium at commercial scale by the end of the decade.

We will end reliance on foreign adversaries for the nuclear fuel powering almost 20% of our grid.

We will fuel advanced reactors and nuclear energy’s growth in the decades ahead.

We are just getting started. Exceptional talent is needed. Join us.
