Meet Sedona: The Neobank Built Around You

发表于2026-04-28|行业观察
|浏览量:

本文转载自 X 博主 @SeismicSys（Seismic） 的长文，原文链接，版权归原作者所有。

Crypto trading platforms and protocols have never been short on innovative ideas. New DeFi primitives, novel yield mechanisms, and increasingly complex onchain architectures have pushed the space forward. But what they have often lacked is the private, simplified, automated, and intuitive user experiences that traditional fintech platforms have spent massive amounts of time and resources streamlining and perfecting.

Sedona was built to deliver this familiar, seamless, user-obsessed experience to decentralized finance for the first time while enabling on-chain privacy for trading, earning, spending, and moving money.

In short, we built Sedona to completely reform the financial experience onchain.

What Is Sedona?

Sedona is a trading-first neobank built on the Seismic ecosystem, which was itself built as a privacy-enabled, compliant blockchain for fintechs. It serves as the market layer and liquidity engine for the network, while introducing something that has been sorely missing from onchain finance: a system that manages your assets simply, predictably, and automatically.

The vision is simple for users. You wake up, open your account, and see that your assets have earned yield overnight. You can move seamlessly between crypto, FX, and other markets, execute trades privately, and spend directly through a card, all within a single application. No fragmentation, no guesswork, and no tradeoffs.

If you have used platforms like Coinbase, Venmo, or Robinhood, the experience will feel instantly recognizable. Clean design, simple actions, clear outcomes, and no unnecessary complexity mean users can trade, earn, and manage their assets onchain just as easily as they could in traditional finance. However, Sedona introduces powerful new earning strategies that traditional fintechs aren’t able to replicate thanks to its blockchain-native engineering.

Visit Sedona

How Sedona Works

Sedona was designed around a straightforward principle: users shouldn’t need to understand blockchain technology in order to manage their money on-chain.

Instead of navigating protocols, contracts, and fragmented liquidity, users interact with a system that behaves consistently and intuitively while handling complexity in the background.

Behind the interface, Seismic provides the infrastructure that makes this possible. Privacy is enforced at the protocol level, compliance is built directly into the network, and on- and off-ramps, along with card integrations, connect the system to real-world financial rails.

When you deposit assets into Sedona, they are automatically routed into the best active markets and rebalanced as conditions change. Yield is generated without manual configuration, and positions are managed continuously without requiring constant user input. Zero-knowledge verification, compliance monitoring, liquidity routing, and private execution all operate behind the scenes to keep your funds secure, encrypted, and living up to their full potential.

The result is a platform where the infrastructure disappears, and the experience remains.

  • Trade: Buy and sell assets with private execution by default. Orders are protected from front-running and information leakage, creating a more stable and predictable trading experience.
  • Earn: Assets are automatically allocated into active markets to generate yield. No setup, no tuning, no strategy management. The system handles it for you.
  • Hold and Spend: Manage your portfolio in one place and spend directly from your balance through integrated financial rails, while your assets continue working in the background.

What to Expect Next From Sedona

Sedona is designed to grow the same way it operates: swiftly and thoughtfully.

The platform will evolve alongside the Seismic network, beginning with early environments and progressing toward broader availability as the infrastructure matures. Features will expand quickly, ensuring that every addition strengthens the reliability and UX of the product rather than complicating it.

Just a trading-first neobank designed to behave exactly like users always wanted.

Welcome to Sedona.
