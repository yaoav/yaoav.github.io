People are getting millions of views with 5-slide TikToks made by AI Agents 🦞

|浏览量:

TL;DR - You can get millions of views by posting slideshows with your AI Agents (OpenClaw / Hermes agent / Claude Cowork / PaperClip / etc.)

(Make sure you save this article so you can read it later.)

**Are you building something right now? Once you finish, what’s next?
**I run Postiz, it’s an agentic social media scheduling tool. It allows you to automate posts to 30+ social media platforms.

I share a lot of my Postiz monthly recurring revenue screenshots on social media (currently making $81k per month), and every time **I get asked: **”What is your distribution method?” My answer is: word-of-mouth. It’s spreading so fast because the people using it drive tons of sales.

One way people generate sales is by using TikTok slideshows.

Postiz grew from $20k → $81k monthly recurring revenue in less than two months. Stripe proof link (cannot be spoofed): https://profile.stripe.com/wickedguro/onem4x95

Back in 2000, running Facebook/Instagram ads was so cheap and had such high conversion rates, but over time, more and more people entered the market, and things became much, much harder.

This article talks about TikTok slideshows. This is a 2026 growth hack. It will not last forever. Over time, more and more people will do it, and it will become less effective. You should at least experiment with it right now.

Growth Hacking is King

In 2026, social media shifted from a follower-based to an interest-based market, which means the platform will try to match your content with people who are interested in it. This is huge because small and mid-size accounts can get huge exposure to their content.

You don’t need to work super hard for years to gain an audience; you can literally get thousands of views from new accounts. Furthermore, some platforms will actually push new accounts’ content more at the start.

And here comes growth hacking - which ones of all those platforms can get you tons of views, conversions, by doing low effort AI Stuff? Well, you guessed it from the title - TikTok.

We are currently seeing an interesting growth hack around the slideshow. You post 4-5 pictures, with text on them, put your custom music, and this can get you tons of people.

It’s not long-form content, but people can spend more time on it, and read things that can make them “buy”, I think it’s a better way to sell.

This is one of the reasons @oliverhenry The article blew up to 7 million views because he drove a lot of sales for his app Snugly by posting only slideshows.

Who’s making money?

@oliverhenry gets thousands of views and sales for his app, Snuggly (~2.5M VIEWS). He cracked what type of slideshow works best, and then let OpenClaw repeat the process again and again.

@mattwelter shared cosmance, Relationship Compatibility app, fully automated slideshows, 4.9M views.

Also audrey.kins

How does it work?

You work together with your agent, which can be OpenClaw / Hermes / Nanoclaw / PaperClip / etc.

You install Oliver’s Larry skill, brainstorm content, and then let OpenClaw create a slideshow and automatically schedule it to social media as a TikTok draft. Inside TikTok, you add your music and then post it. Music is usually one of the most important things.

Once you have found a winning format, you can set a cron (or routing, depending on your agents) to keep on posting the same thing and double down on it.

Once it’s stopped working, you can try new formats.
Later, you can open multiple TikTok accounts and post to all of them.

The whole process will use:

  • Your agent (OpenClaw / Claude / Hermes / Nanoclaw / PaperClip / etc.)
  • OpenAI to generate your pictures. In the skill, we use OpenAI image-1.5
  • Some Python libraries to add text overlay to the slideshow
  • Postiz or an equivalent - it’s a service that allows you to schedule posts to social media to one or more accounts at scale.

So what should you do?

  • Create a new TikTok account or buy a warmed one. warmed accounts are still accounts with no followers, but they were created in the US and have some engagement there. check superwarm
  • Make sure you have disconnected your GPS from your phone and are using a US VPN. I am using NordVPN with a dedicated IP. Make sure you don’t use a datacenter up. Generally speaking, if you can, always try to have a US mobile connection. It can be better to buy a $50 android phone from Amazon. If you even once open your phone in your country, it can start showing content to your local audience.
  • Go like, comment, and save posts from similar content creators. THIS IS A MUST. If you don’t do it, the algorithm won’t show your post to the right people.
  • Register for a free 7-day trial for Postiz or an equivalent
  • Install the Postiz CLI (you can also read the full docs here):
  • Use the official Postiz Skill to schedule posts, or if you want to create slideshows, use @oliverhenry Larry’s skill to schedule slideshows.

Some viral slide-show hooks you can use

  • First slide -> X amount of things to get Y, every other thing should be numbered.

  • First slide -> Hook, write a problem that many people face -> every slide shows a solution to the problem

  • First slide -> Hook, Information that I am the only one who knows -> every slide gives information

  • First slide: My [Insert] says I am this; other slides show the fix by downloading an app. Here is what @oliverhenry said

**Hooks nobody cared about:
**
→ “Why does my flat look like a student loan” → 905 views
→ “See your room in 12+ styles before you commit” → 879 views
→ “The difference between $500 and $5000 taste” → 2,671 views
None of them worked.
We were talking about our problems and our app. Nobody cared.

Then we tried:
→ “My landlord said I can’t change anything so I showed her what AI thinks it could look like”
**234,000 views.
**
It worked because it wasn’t about us. It was about someone else’s reaction: a landlord, a conflict, then changing their mind.
We repeated it:

→ “I showed my mum what AI thinks our living room could be” → 167,000 views
→ “My landlord wouldn’t let me decorate until I showed her these” → 147,000 views

The formula was simple:
[Another person] + [conflict or doubt] → showed them AI → they changed their mind

Posts with this formula hit 50K+ consistently. Most pass 100K. Everything else struggles to reach 10K.
