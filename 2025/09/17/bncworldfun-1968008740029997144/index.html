Introducing $NEMESIS

发表于2025-09-17|行业观察
|浏览量:

Nemesis

In the ever-evolving landscape of crypto, Nemesis is building the decentralized exchange (DEX) for Internet Capital Markets. Built to address the long-overdue problem of short-selling onchain, Nemesis creates Omni-directional Market Maker, a disruptive liquidity framework that enables seamless swapping, short-selling, and market-making—all within a single, decentralized liquidity pool. Free from reliance on derivatives or centralized components, Nemesis embodies the true spirit of DeFi: decentralized, permissionless, transparent, and innovative.

At its core, Nemesis is designed to foster viral growth and genuine adoption by democratizing access to novel defi primitives. Whether you’re a retail trader seeking embedded leverage or a project builder looking to launch tokenized assets, Nemesis delivers a non-derivative architecture that upholds decentralization while unlocking new onchain opportunities. As we embark on this journey, we invite the community to join us in building a more inclusive financial future—one where decentralization and permissionlessness become the backbone of global capital flows.

Internet Capital Markets

Internet Capital Markets (ICM) represent a transformative shift in how capital is raised, allocated, and traded, leveraging blockchain technology to create open, internet-native financial systems. Unlike traditional capital markets bogged down by intermediaries, regulations, and geographic barriers, ICM enables anyone with an internet connection to issue tokens, raise funds, or invest in ideas and assets directly onchain. This paradigm democratizes finance by tokenizing everything—from real-world assets (RWAs) like equities and commodities to intangible value like attention, social influence, or even brands—turning the internet into a borderless marketplace.

We believe this new paradigm is going to disrupt legacy systems and drive exponential growth in crypto. The high throughput and low costs of blockchains such as Solana and Base have already positioned us to tokenize everything of value and embrace a new trillion-dollar scale market, the market for internet capital formation. We are witnessing a world where early-stage investments are accessible to all, barriers to entry vanish, and global participation fuels innovation. Imagine tokenizing personal achievements, community-driven projects, or even memes with real economic backing—ICM could evolve into the ultimate engine for viral, inclusive growth, blending social dynamics with blockchain to redefine wealth creation. As a DEX that enables swap, short selling, and liquidity provision and unified liquidity across trading and lending protocols, Nemesis is positioned to power this paradigm shift.

As the first token of Nemesis, $NEMESIS enables everyone to become an early participant in Nemesis’s long-term development and share in the value accrual of this trillion-dollar market.

Introducing $NEMESIS

What is $NEMESIS?

$NEMESIS is the first token of Nemesis. It is also the first ICM token on Base with long-term value.

It has two core utilities:

  • It allows you to partake in Nemesis’s long-term development from product to community to brand, owning $NEMESIS is owning a piece of Nemesis. And since we are doing a fair launch, everyone is getting the same valuation, whether you are a crypto newbie, a degen, a whale, or a top institutional investor.
  • It allows you to partake in this paradigm shift of internet finance, owning $NEMESIS is owning a piece of this trillion-dollar market, but from its nascence.

Why Base?

We have chosen Base to launch $NEMESIS and Nemesis because it empowers DeFi protocols with exceptional scalability, delivering transaction speeds up to 10x faster and fees 10x lower than Ethereum mainnet, which minimizes slippage in high-frequency trading on DEXes and makes liquidity provision accessible for retail users without prohibitive costs. We have also considered Solana, but its increasing competition and gradually established market landscape in the DEX space did not make it our first choice.

Security-wise, Base inherits Ethereum’s robust security model, including fraud proofs and shared sequencing, ensuring that DeFi applications handling billions in TVL—such as DEXs and lending protocols—remain protected against exploits while supporting various, complex smart contract interactions. Meanwhile, its full EVM compatibility is friendly to cross-chain protocols like Nemesis, with fast block times (around 2 seconds) enabling real-time oracle updates and arbitrage opportunities, while interoperability via the Superchain and cross-chain bridges facilitates efficient asset transfers across ecosystems, boosting liquidity pools and reducing fragmentation in DeFi composability. Lastly, developer tools like Hardhat and integrated audits streamline the creation of secure, gas-optimized protocols, fostering innovation in areas like flash loans and tokenized real-world assets. All of these features are exactly what Nemesis needs with its early-stage protocol development.

Ecosystem-wise, incubated by Coinbase, Base benefits from deep integration with one of the largest crypto platforms, providing seamless access to over 110 million verified users through easy fiat onramps, wallet integrations, and acquisition tools. We believe Base’s Coinbase backing offers it superior user onboarding and liquidity, and will contribute to Nemesis’s future growth in TVL and transaction volume.

Lastly, as the Base creator Jesse Pollak mentioned at Base Camp 2025, the network is officially exploring the launch of a native token to enhance decentralization, support global adoption, and foster creator growth within its ecosystem. We think this will drive more user and developer activities onchain, and thus benefit Nemesis and its early users in the long term.

At Nemesis, we decide to launch an Internet Capital Markets (ICM) token on Base to foster long-term builders and projects with genuine utility, and shifts ICM from short-term speculation—common on chains like Solana—to enduring value creation, attracting serious builders with tools for app monetization, regulatory clarity, and global reach, ultimately democratizing fundraising while prioritizing utility over fleeting trends.

Where Are We Launching $NEMESIS?

We will launch $NEMESIS on District.xyz, a launchpad built for true builders. Backed by Andreessen Horowitz, Initialized Capital, SV Angel, and many other renowned investors, District is positioned to be a leading launchpad on Base for long-term builders and projects. As the first ICM token launched on District, we believe this strategic partnership will ensure a highly successful debut for $NEMESIS and ignite a transformative paradigm shift.

What’s Next?

The era of Internet Capital Markets is here. Nemesis and $NEMESIS are at its center.

Stay tuned for updates and follow @nemesisdottrade on X for the latest news.
